The essentials

“Carbine” is one of the 2 values of the Tactical flashlight slot (weapon customization). Series benchmark: 375 $ in GTA V; this is not a GTA VI price, as nothing has been published. Note: $1,675 to $2,350 online. The other values of the slot: Pistol (472 $).
