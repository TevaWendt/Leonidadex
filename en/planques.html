Official sources

What Rockstar has shown

A safehouse, a boatyard, a motel: three places shown, then edition texts that finally describe the system.

  1. December 4, 2023Seen in media

    Trailer 1

    A motel room, curtain drawn: Jason and Lucia at the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn (around 1:12). The sign, readable in the 2025 screenshots, made it possible to place it on the map.

  2. May 6, 2025Seen in media

    Trailer 2 and screenshots

    Brian’s boatyard, with the Brian’s Boat Works & Marina sign readable at night around 1:25, and Jason’s fight in the Starlet parking lot around 1:54. A motel room shows up again around 2:25.

  3. June 24, 2026Official

    Editions

    Rockstar calls the house in the Keys “Jason’s Safehouse” and describes two garages: Paradise in Watson Bay (Ultimate Edition, with the Dominator Buggy ’67) and Shore Court, “a stone’s throw from Ocean Beach” (Vintage Vice City Pack, with the Stanier ’55). Each one has a weapon locker and a stash for stolen goods, which a local fence buys at a discount. Personal garages exist “throughout Leonida”.

  4. August 27, 2026Seen in media

    Extended Look and demo

    The Starlet Motel shows up again around 25:58. The next day, Rob Nelson (Rockstar North) told Famitsu that you change safehouses as the chapters go on, that you start with three vehicle slots and buy garages to get more; long guns go in a locker at Ammu-Nation.

The two edition garages are documented above, under the pages, with their source and a box to check when you get them.

05

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

How many safehouses are in GTA VI?
No number published. Only one address officially carries the safehouse name, “Jason’s Safehouse”; Rob Nelson told Famitsu that you change safehouses as the chapters go on. The lists with three safehouses come from fan sites.
Can you buy garages?
Yes, according to Rob Nelson (Famitsu, August 28, 2026): you start with three vehicle slots and buy garages on the map to get more. No price is published.
What’s in a personal garage?
According to the edition texts (June 24, 2026): a weapon locker to put together your loadout and a stash to drop off stolen goods, which a local fence then buys at a discount.
Is the Starlet Motel a safehouse?
It’s shown in Trailer 1, Trailer 2 and the Extended Look, always with Jason and Lucia. Rockstar has never called it a safehouse: the site lists it as “seen in media”, without claiming anything more.
Where are Watson Bay and Shore Court?
Watson Bay is home to the Ultimate Edition’s Paradise garage; Shore Court is “a stone’s throw from Ocean Beach” according to the Vintage Pack text. Rockstar hasn’t given an exact address, so they’re not placed on the map yet.

Sources and statuses

Every claim on this page has a status and comes from a page opened on the date shown. Rockstar pages are cited with the date they were last opened directly; their texts were reread on September 27, 2026 in the sources that reproduce them.

Official

A page or video published by Rockstar (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look), or statements by a Rockstar representative reported by a named outlet, with the date.

Seen in media

What an official image or clip shows without any Rockstar text saying it: a trailer shot, a screenshot, a moment from the Extended Look, with the timestamp when known.

Community identification

A match made by players, a wiki or a fan site (real place, actor, county). Quoted as such, never presented as confirmed.

To be confirmed

A question with no published answer: price, purchase, access, role in the story. We write it down so we don’t guess it.
