Overview

The Final Chapter MC is the motorcycle club of Ambrosia, the region of the Allied Crystal sugar refinery. Rockstar doesn’t name it in its texts, but sums up the region in one line: the refinery provides the jobs, a local biker gang provides pretty much everything else. The club’s name can be read on the region’s first official screenshot, on the back of the vests: “Final Chapter” on the top rocker, “Ambrosia” on the bottom one, “1982” and “MC” on either side of a hooded skull holding an open book. A second screenshot shows a bearded man with an “Enforcer” patch, a common title in motorcycle clubs, with a woman biker hanging on his neck and brandishing a Mustang .357 revolver.

What official media show

In the screenshot, a procession of bikers on chrome cruisers rides up an Ambrosia shopping street under American flags, past a Xero gas station and a sign pointing to the Ambrosia County Sheriff’s Office. The vests are black, the patches yellow and black. Nothing in the image says whether the club will be an ally, an employer or a target for Jason and Lucia.

What we know for the game

Rockstar hasn’t published anything about the club’s role in the story, or about its hideouts or exact territory. Rob Nelson explained to Famitsu that you can scope out gang hideouts, break in and steal money or drugs; it isn’t known yet whether the Final Chapter’s hideouts are among them. The Western Sovereign motorcycles from the screenshot and the Mustang .357 revolver each have their own page.

Key points

  • The name can be read on the vests in an official Ambrosia screenshot: “Final Chapter” at the top, “Ambrosia” at the bottom, “1982” and “MC” on the sides.
  • Its emblem is a hooded skull holding an open book in skeletal hands.
  • Rockstar describes a local biker gang that provides Ambrosia with pretty much everything the refinery doesn’t.
  • Another screenshot of the region shows a biker with an “Enforcer” patch and a woman biker armed with a Mustang .357 revolver.
