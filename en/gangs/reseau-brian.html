Smuggling network, GTA VI

Brian Heder’s network

Old-school smuggling, out of the Keys.

The Keys drug runners Jason works for, centered on Brian Heder, a smuggler from the golden age who moves his goods through his boatyard.

Overview

Before he meets Lucia, Jason already has an employer: the Keys drug runners. Rockstar shows him working for them because it’s what he does best, and names the oldest of them, Brian Heder, who learned the trade during the golden age of smuggling. Brian still moves his goods through his boatyard with Lori, his third wife, and now lets others do the dirty work. He puts Jason up for free in exchange for help with local debt collection. Cal Hampton, Jason’s friend and Brian’s associate, moves in the same circle. Rockstar gives this group no name other than local drug runners.

What official media show

Trailer 2 shows the boatyard at night, with the Brian’s Boat Works & Marina sign lit up, while cargo is being loaded. An official screenshot shows Brian, a white plastic-wrapped package in hand, giving instructions to a man in a helmet; others show him at his dock or behind the wheel of his pickup. In the Extended Look, he has Jason and Lucia over for dinner to hand them a job.

What we know for the game

It isn’t known yet which missions the network hands out, or how they tie in with Raul’s or Boobie’s. The boatyard and Jason’s house in the Keys are marked on the map; this page links them, along with Brian’s Caracara 4x4 pickup.

Key points

  • Jason works for local drug runners in the Keys, according to his official introduction.
  • Their known face is Brian Heder, who learned the trade during the golden age of smuggling in the archipelago.
  • The goods move through Brian’s boatyard, which he runs with Lori, his third wife.
  • In the Extended Look, Brian hands Jason and Lucia a job over dinner.
