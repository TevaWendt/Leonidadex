Street gang · GTA VI

San4San

A name read by players, never introduced by Rockstar.

A Vice City gang identified by players: its name can reportedly be read on a chain and a mural in the trailers. Rockstar has never introduced it in any text.

View on the map

Overview

San4San is a name players read in the official GTA VI videos, without Rockstar ever having written it in a text. According to the GTA Wiki and GTABase, a man wears a San4San chain at the modded car meet in Trailer 1, around 1:08, and the name comes back on a mural in the La Perle neighborhood in Trailer 2, around 0:46. The wiki also links it to the dealers at Venture Apartments, in the Rockridge neighborhood, where Jason and Lucia come to pick up a delivery on Boobie Ike’s behalf at the start of the Extended Look. The scene is cut short: an armed police raid forces them to flee.

What official media show

The August 27, 2026 Extended Look opens with this scene: a Vice City apartment building, lookouts at the foot of the stairs, a contact Jason asks for by the name Raymond whose partner has disappeared, then an armed police unit showing up. The video doesn’t name the group: the link to San4San comes from players.

What we know for the game

As long as Rockstar says nothing about it, this page stays a community identification: no official territory, no leader, no known role in the story. It will be corrected, or removed, depending on what the game shows at release.
