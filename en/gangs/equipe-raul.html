Raul Bautista’s crew

Keep up, or get out.

The robbers recruited by Raul Bautista, who’s always on the lookout for talent ready to take the biggest risks. In the Extended Look, Jason and Lucia join it after a successful getaway.

Overview

Raul Bautista never works alone. His official introduction describes him as always looking for partners ready to take the risks that pay big, and warns that his taste for danger raises the stakes with every job: the people around him will have to keep up, or walk away. The August 27, 2026 Extended Look shows how you get in. Jason and Lucia act as getaway drivers after one of Raul’s heists; impressed by their driving, he brings them into his crew. Rockstar gives it no name other than his: so this page talks about Raul’s crew.

What official media show

The official screenshots show Raul on the phone in front of surveillance screens, at the wheel with a pistol marked CAPO, on the deck of a boat facing the Vice City towers, and in front of a bar’s neon lights. The other crew members are neither shown nor named.

What we know for the game

We don’t know how many jobs the crew is planning, or how the loot is split. Rob Nelson told Famitsu that a jewelry store or a small bank can be robbed solo or as a pair, and that missions alone aren’t enough to fund the story: money earned in the open world is what moves it forward. The calculator helps you estimate what a job needs to pay to be worth the time spent.

Key points

  • Its leader, Raul Bautista, is introduced by Rockstar as a seasoned bank robber who’s always recruiting.
  • Rockstar warns that each of Raul’s jobs is riskier than the last: his partners keep up, or leave.
  • In the Extended Look, Jason and Lucia drive the getaway car after one of Raul’s heists, and he takes them into his crew.
  • The crew has no published name.
