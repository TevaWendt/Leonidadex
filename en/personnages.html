Characters · GTA VI

The characters of GTA VI

Who are Jason, Lucia and the people who cross their path? These profiles link the characters presented by Rockstar to the people around them and to Leonida’s regions.

Explore their connections and the official scenes from each page. Being in the story doesn’t mean a character will be playable; the descriptions stick to published information. Understanding statuses.

The 8 pages

Character

Jason Duval

He’d like an easy life; things just keep getting more complicated.

A former soldier raised among grifters, Jason works for traffickers in the Keys, which is what he does best. He’d like an easy life; meeting Lucia is going to complicate everything.

Cal Hampton

What if everything you read online was true?

Jason’s friend and Brian’s associate, Cal feels safest at home, listening in on Coast Guard radio with a few beers and some private browsing tabs open. What if everything you read online was true?

Boobie Ike

It’s all about heart: the Jack of Hearts.

A local Vice City legend, Boobie turned his years on the street into a legit empire: real estate, the Jack of Hearts club and the Only Raw Records label, set up with Dre’Quan. All they’re missing is a hit.

Dre’Quan Priest

Only Raw… Records.

More hustler than gangster, Dre’Quan has always had his eye on music. He signed Real Dimez, teamed up with Boobie in Only Raw Records and fully intends to take over the whole Vice City scene.

Real Dimez

Viral videos, viral hooks.

Bae-Luxe and Roxy, friends since high school, turned their hustles into rap tracks and a nonstop online presence. Signed to Only Raw Records after a first hit, they’re hoping lightning strikes twice.

Raul Bautista

Experience counts.

A seasoned bank robber, Raul recruits partners ready to take the risks that pay the most. His recklessness raises the stakes with every job; his crew will have to keep up or get out.

Brian Heder

Nothing beats a Mudslide at sunset.

An old-school trafficker from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys, Brian still moves his goods through his boatyard with Lori, his third wife. He puts Jason up in exchange for a few favors.

Official sources

What Rockstar has shown

Eight profiles, three videos, a few interviews: what each official reveal says about them.

  1. December 4, 2023Seen in media

    Trailer 1

    Lucia kicks things off, in an orange jumpsuit, in an interview at the Leonida Penitentiary; then with Jason in a room at the Starlet Motel and during the robbery of an Uncle Jack’s Liquor store. Jason isn’t named yet.

  2. May 6, 2025Official

    Eight “Only in Leonida” profiles and Trailer 2

    Rockstar names Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, the ones you play, then Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre’Quan Priest, Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy), Raul Bautista and Brian Heder, each with a tagline. Trailer 2 shows Jason on his roof in the Keys, Brian sending him out to collect, Jason picking Lucia up when she gets out of prison (0:52), and Cal at the Rusty Anchor (1:29).

  3. June 24, 2026Official

    Editions

    The edition texts talk about the duo in the game’s present: outfits, tattoos and hairstyles “for Jason and Lucia”, “Jason’s” pickup and safehouse. No new characters.

  4. August 27, 2026Seen in media

    Extended Look

    Four missions: a retrieval job for Boobie that goes wrong, a meeting with Brian and Lori at the Effluvia bar (10:50), Lucia driving for Raul during a chase, and a shootout at the Megamundo opening. The game switches from Jason to Lucia mid-chase (18:41). Cal wraps it up with a monologue.

Rob Nelson, co-director of Rockstar North, speaking to the press between August 27 and September 4, 2026: the romance is optional; switching characters is sometimes up to you, sometimes forced; four characters were considered before the duo; his own playthrough took about 80 hours, optional content included.

05

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Who is Lucia Caminos?
One of the two characters you play, and the series’ first female lead. Her official profile (May 6, 2025): her father taught her to fight, defending her family sent her to the penitentiary, and she comes out determined to make only smart moves.
Who is Jason Duval?
The other playable character: a former soldier raised among grifters, he lives in the Keys at Brian Heder’s place and works for the local traffickers.
How many playable characters are in GTA VI?
Two: Jason and Lucia. Rob Nelson explained in September 2026 that four characters were considered before the duo. The other six aren’t announced as playable.
Who voices Lucia and Jason?
Rockstar hasn’t published anything. Fans name Manni L. Perez for Lucia and Dylan Rourke for Jason; only Stephen Root has confirmed a role, without saying which one.
Can you switch characters at any time?
Not always. The Extended Look shows a switch from Jason to Lucia mid-chase; Rob Nelson told Famitsu that the story sometimes picks the character for you.
Are Jason and Lucia a couple?
It’s up to you: Rob Nelson told IGN that the romance is optional. Its effects on the game aren’t detailed.

Sources and statuses

Every claim on this page has a status and comes from a page opened on the date shown. Rockstar pages are cited with the date they were last opened directly; their texts were reread on September 27, 2026 in the sources that reproduce them.

Official

A page or video published by Rockstar (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look), or statements by a Rockstar representative reported by a named outlet, with the date.

Seen in media

What an official image or clip shows without any Rockstar text saying it: a trailer shot, a screenshot, a moment from the Extended Look, with the timestamp when known.

Community identification

A match made by players, a wiki or a fan site (real place, actor, county). Quoted as such, never presented as confirmed.

To be confirmed

A question with no published answer: price, purchase, access, role in the story. We write it down so we don’t guess it.
