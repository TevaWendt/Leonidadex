Vehicle customization, Paint

Primary paint

Primary paint in GTA VI: Paint, series benchmark. Classic, metallic, matte, metal or chrome: the main body color.

Series benchmarkPaint
In the list

The essentials

Classic, metallic, matte, metal or chrome: the main body color. Status: series benchmark (seen in another game, not yet in GTA VI). Effect: cosmetic only. Series benchmark, GTA V: from 400 $ to 1,200 $ (classic $400, metallic $650, metal $900, matte $1,000, chrome $1,200); this is not a GTA VI price. Where to find it: Customization workshop (benchmark: Los Santos Customs, GTA V). Options recorded in GTA V (5 values, one page each): Classic (400 $), Metallic (650 $), Metal (900 $), Matte (1,000 $), Chrome (1,200 $). Vehicle categories concerned: sedans, sports cars, supercars, muscle cars, SUV, pickups, vans, service vehicles, miscellaneous vehicles, motorcycles. Variants: classic, metallic, matte, metal, chrome.
