        Frequently asked questions

        Why is the list empty?

        Because no collectible has been documented by a reliable source yet. The tracker will fill up as soon as the first pages arrive, and you won’t lose what you’ve already checked.

        Are my collectibles saved online?

        No. They stay in this browser. The export on the Progress page and the one in the collectibles catalog take them along.

        Is the catalog’s tracker the same as this one?

        They read the same list. The catalog is for searching and planning an outing; this tracker shows at a glance what you’ve found.
