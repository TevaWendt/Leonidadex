      How this tracker works

      Tasted or in stock

      “Tasted” counts toward your progress. Stock is noted separately, only if you want: how many you have left. A stock at zero doesn’t uncheck anything.

      A box checked earlier

      A consumable checked before stock tracking arrived shows “stock not set”. The site never guesses a quantity: enter it when you know it.

      What’s counted

      The 29 tracked consumables, in 5 categories. Exact prices and effects are waiting for the game: they’re “to be confirmed” in the list.

      • Drinks 7
      • Snacks 5
      • Meals 9
      • Protection 6
      • Healing 2

      Frequently asked questions

      Can “I used one” count twice?

      No. Each stock update is saved only once, even if you click twice or the page is open in two tabs: the tracker rereads your stock before writing.

      Does the calculator remove items from my stock?

      No. A calculator plan is a forecast: it never touches what you’ve noted here. Only what you say you’ve done changes your stock.

      Is my stock saved online?

      No. It stays in this browser. The export on the Progress page takes it along with the rest of your progress.
