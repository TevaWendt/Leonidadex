      How this tracker works

      01

      Three families

      Weapons (27 weapons), gear (16 gear items) and ammo types (5 ammo types). The “Family” filter shows only one of them.

      02

      Obtained, then in stock

      A checked ammo type stays obtained. Stock is noted separately: you enter how much you have left, then “I used one” or “I bought another one” updates it. We never guess a quantity.

      03

      Where to add it

      On each weapon page, “Add to my arsenal”. In the Armory, “I have it” on each gear item and each ammo type. Here, in “Not in my arsenal yet”.

      Frequently asked questions

      Why does my ammo say “stock not set”?

      You checked this ammo type before stock tracking arrived, or you haven’t noted it yet. The site never makes up a quantity: enter it when you know it. Until then, the type still counts as obtained.

      Is my arsenal saved online?

      No. It stays in this browser. The export on the Progress page takes it along with the rest of your progress, stock included.

      Does a wish count toward my progress?

      No. A wish is a weapon you want: it doesn’t check anything. When you get it, “I have it” puts it in your arsenal and removes it from your wishlist.
