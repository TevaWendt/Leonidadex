      What’s counted

      Documented content you can check off: 4 for now. The Ultimate Edition boats are vehicles: they count in your garage, never twice.

      Housing, apartments, safehouses and residences for sale: none has been published as buyable. They don’t count, and nothing is made up in their place.

      Frequently asked questions

      Why aren’t my boats here?

      Because they’re vehicles: they’re in your garage. Each item counts only once in your progress.

      Why is there no house to check off?

      Rockstar hasn’t published any buyable property. Residences and safehouses have their pages, but nothing says you can buy them: so we don’t check them off.

      Is my content saved online?

      No. It stays in this browser. The export on the Progress page takes it along.
