      How this tracker works

      Owned, wanted, not yet

      “In my garage” counts what you own. “My wishlist” keeps what you want, without counting it. “Not in my garage yet” lists every cataloged vehicle you haven’t checked.

      Where to add it

      On each page, the “Add to my garage” button. On the Vehicles page, the “Garage” box on each card. Here, right in “Not in my garage yet”.

      What’s counted

      Frequently asked questions

      Is my garage saved online?

      No. Your garage stays in this browser, on this device. To keep it or get it back somewhere else, export your progress from the Progress page: you get a file you import on the other device.

      What’s the difference between “owned” and “wish”?

      An owned vehicle counts toward your progress. A wish doesn’t count: it’s a vehicle you want. The calculator can then rank your wishes so you know what to buy first.

      Why is a vehicle in “Leftover entries”?

      It was checked on this device, but its ID no longer exists in the list (page renamed or removed). It stays saved, doesn’t count, and you can remove it.
