      How this tracker works

      01

      One calculation, one summary

      Each saved item keeps its one-sentence summary. “Open the calculation sheet” takes you to it in the calculator, and nothing is replaced until you confirm.

      02

      Planned isn’t done

      A plan is a forecast: it doesn’t check anything in your trackers. When you log a session you played and a purchase you made, the plan can add it to your garage or your arsenal, just once.

      03

      Rename, duplicate, delete

      You do these in the calculator, in the “My saved calculations” tab. This tracker reads them without ever changing them.

      Frequently asked questions

      Are my calculations saved online?

      No. They stay in this browser, up to 160 saved items. The export on the Progress page takes them along.

      Does a plan check vehicles in my garage?

      Not while it’s a forecast. When you note in the plan that a purchase is done, the matching vehicle or weapon is added to your tracker, just once, even if you save twice.

      Why won’t a calculation open anymore?

      It may have been deleted in the calculator or in another tab. Reload the page: the tracker rereads the up-to-date list.
