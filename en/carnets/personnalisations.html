      Frequently asked questions

      Is an installed mod tied to a specific vehicle?

      No. The tracker notes that you’ve installed that type of mod, not on which vehicle. Compatibility details are in the list row.

      Are my customizations saved online?

      No. They stay in this browser. The export on the Progress page takes them along.

      Why an icon instead of a photo?

      When no official image shows the mod, the thumbnail is its category icon. It’s not a picture of the item.
