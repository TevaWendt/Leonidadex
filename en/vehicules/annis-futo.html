Overview

The Futo is the ultimate lightweight rear-wheel-drive car, the one you pick to learn how to drift rather than to go fast. The Toyota AE86, whose shape it borrows, has a huge aura: an affordable, well-balanced car, it became the symbol of amateur drifting before manga culture made it famous. Trailer 1 shows it in the background, taillights on.

Where its design comes from

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Toyota AE86 Levin. Rockstar designs replicas, never licensed models, and doesn’t say which real models they’re based on. This match is still just an observation.
