What the artwork shows

The Crown Victoria in taxi livery, with checkers down the sides and a sign on the roof. It’s the ultimate fleet sedan: huge trunk, ladder-frame chassis, bulletproof mechanicals that are cheap to maintain. Trailer 1 shows it driving through Vice City traffic.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Ford Crown Victoria. The studio holds no license and names no reference. This match is a deduction drawn from the images.
