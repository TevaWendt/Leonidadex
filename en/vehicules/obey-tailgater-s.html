What you need to know

A longer, better-equipped version of the Tailgater, along the lines of an Audi A4 versus an A6: same design language, more length, more discreet chrome. It’s the executive sedan, the one with a chauffeur. The official screenshots single it out, sitting on its stock wheels.

For context

The Obey Tailgater S is a vehicle in the sedans category, listed on Leonidakit. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. It comes from the official screenshot gallery, the most precise source for identifying a model. It’s a European model, like most of the series’ luxury sedans and supercars.

The numbers arrive on November 19, 2026: price, top speed, acceleration, capacity and where it’s sold, all measured in the game.

The real-world model

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Audi RS3 Sedan. No license is involved here. The studio doesn’t confirm its sources, and this match comes from looking at the published images.
