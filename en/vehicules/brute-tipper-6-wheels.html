What the artwork shows

Brute’s six-wheel dump truck, built on a Kodiak and TopKick base: two rear axles, a tipping bed, and a size somewhere between a light-duty truck and a heavy truck. The Extended Look shows it driving fully loaded on a dirt road at a construction site.

The real-world match

The match chosen for this vehicle is Chevrolet Kodiak, GMC TopKick. The studio doesn’t use any license and doesn’t name any reference. This match is a deduction drawn from the images.
