What the artwork shows

The police helicopter, with a searchlight and a camera. It shows up at high wanted levels and adds pressure from above that you can’t answer if you stay on the ground.

For context

The Buckingham Police Maverick belongs to the helicopters category of the catalog. The vehicle appears in media released by Rockstar, but its name hasn’t been announced: the one shown here is a match with the series or the real-world model. Its first appearance goes back to Trailer 1, the one that revealed Leonida and Vice City.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Eurocopter AS350B. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on the real models it starts from.

See photos of the Eurocopter AS350BEncyclopedia page

Its place in a GTA

The series’ helicopters open up the map in one go: no more roads, no more traffic jams, a view over the whole state. They fly with precision, and you rarely find one sitting by the side of the street.
