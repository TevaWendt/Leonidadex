What the artwork shows

A more hardcore version of the Coquette, in the spirit of the Corvette Z06: wider track, bold aero, sharper engine. The Z06 has traditionally been the version Chevrolet builds for the track while keeping it street-legal. Trailer 1 has it burst into a night shot.

For context

The Invetero Coquette D10 is in the catalog’s Sports cars category. Officially sighted, name deduced: this page keeps the most likely name and will change it as soon as Rockstar publishes one. It appears in Trailer 1 (December 2023), the game’s very first public reveal. The inspiration is American: the series has always drawn on Detroit automakers and their spin-offs.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Chevrolet Corvette C8. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on the models it starts from.

Its place in a GTA

The series’ sports cars sit between the sedan and the supercar: livelier, lower, often two-door, without the price tag of the exotic models.
