What the artwork shows

Rockstar named this vehicle in the Ultimate Edition reveal, and our four official screenshots show it from every angle: blue body with a yellow stripe, number 6, tractor tires, a roof bar with four spotlights, a mesh grille behind a bull bar. The interior keeps the original three-spoke steering wheel and speedometer. A 1967 Ford Mustang, lifted and built for mud: on its own, it sums up rural swamp Florida, a few miles from the towers of Vice City.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle is the 1967 Ford Mustang, built for off-road. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on its starting models.
