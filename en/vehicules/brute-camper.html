What you need to know

Converted leisure van with a pop-up roof and a fitted-out interior. An archetype tied to the coast and beach life, so it’s right at home in Leonida.

The real-world model

The match we picked for this vehicle is the 1996-2002 Chevrolet Express. Rockstar never confirms its inspirations: this is an observation based on the official images, not a license.
