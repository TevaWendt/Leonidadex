What you need to know

The police version of the Buzzard, with a livery close to that of the Miami Police Department’s aircraft. The MH-6 Little Bird it’s modeled on is a light helicopter of military origin, picked by city police forces for its small size and modest operating costs.

The real-world model

The match chosen for this vehicle is the MH-6 Little Bird. This is a visual comparison, not official information: the studio doesn’t comment on its inspirations.
