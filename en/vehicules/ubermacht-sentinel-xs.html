Overview

A modern take on the Sentinel, modeled on the 3 Series of the 2000s and 2010s. A rear-wheel-drive compact coupe, integrated bumpers, a sharp shoulder line. The benchmark for the mass-market German sports sedan.

Where its design comes from

The match chosen for this vehicle is the BMW M3 E92. The studio stays quiet about its references. This match is based on comparing the official images, nothing more.
