The essentials

The Rebel is modeled on the Toyota Hilux, a pickup so famous for being indestructible that it became the go-to vehicle for conflict zones and expeditions. Its mechanical simplicity is exactly what makes it valuable. It shows up in the official screenshots.

In a nutshell

Pickups and off-roaders: that’s where the Karin Rebel sits in our database. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name we use is temporary and will change to match the studio’s first mention. It can be seen in one or more screenshots from the official gallery on rockstargames.com. A Japanese-made model, in line with a series tradition going back to the 2000s.

What’s still missing (price, performance, where to buy it, what you can modify) will come from data recorded in the game, not estimates.

The model that inspires it

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Toyota Hilux. The studio stays quiet about its references. This match is based on comparing the official images, nothing more.

What this kind of vehicle is for

The series’ pickups have a bed, generous ground clearance and often four-wheel drive. They’re the vehicles of the countryside, construction sites and swamps.
