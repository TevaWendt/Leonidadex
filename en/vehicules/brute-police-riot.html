The essentials

The armored riot-control truck shows up on screen, but its name is still unknown. These vehicles, often military surplus handed down to local police, have become a topic of public debate in the United States. Their presence in a city like Vice City is no small detail.

The model that inspires it

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Lenco BEAR. Rockstar designs look-alikes, never licensed models, and doesn’t say which ones. This match is still just an observation.
