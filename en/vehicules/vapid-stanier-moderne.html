Overview

The modern Stanier, not to be confused with the Ultimate Edition’s Stanier ’55. It’s based on the Ford Crown Victoria, the rear-wheel-drive, body-on-frame sedan that served as America’s police car and taxi for more than twenty years. Indestructible, heavy, and instantly recognizable.

In a nutshell

On Leonidakit, the Vapid Stanier is listed among the sedans. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. It appears in Trailer 1 from December 2023, the game’s very first public reveal. The inspiration is American: the series has always drawn on Detroit automakers and their spin-offs.

Price, speed, acceleration, where it’s sold and customization options will be added to this page after release, from data recorded in the game.

Where its design comes from

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Ford Crown Victoria. Rockstar designs replicas, never licensed models, and doesn’t say which ones. This match is still just an observation.

This type of vehicle, in the game

In the series, a sedan is the vehicle nobody notices: four doors, a trunk, the look of a family car or a company fleet car, which makes it a low-key way to get around.
