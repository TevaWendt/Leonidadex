Overview

A tube-frame off-road buggy, the Outlaw sits somewhere between a quad and a car: full roll cage, two seats side by side, long-travel suspension. The buggy appears in Trailer 1.

Where its design comes from

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Can-Am Maverick X3 Turbo. The studio stays quiet about its references. This match is based on comparing the official images, nothing more.
