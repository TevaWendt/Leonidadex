The essentials

A short off-roader with exposed roll bars, modeled on the current Wrangler Rubicon. The Wrangler is still one of the few mainstream 4x4s to keep solid axles and a removable roof, which explains why you see it all over Florida’s wetlands. Seen in the Extended Look.

In a nutshell

SUVs and 4x4s category: that’s where the Canis Terminus sits in our database. The vehicle appears in media released by Rockstar, but its name hasn’t been announced: the one shown here is a match with the series or the real-world model. It shows up in the Extended Look, the game’s detailed showcase. Its real-world model is American, which fits the vibe of a Florida-inspired state.

What’s still missing (price, performance, where to buy it, what you can modify) will come from data recorded in the game, not estimates.

The model that inspires it

The match chosen for this vehicle is the fourth-generation Jeep Wrangler Rubicon. Nobody at Rockstar has confirmed anything. The identification comes from comparing it with the published artwork.
