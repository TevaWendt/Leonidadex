What the artwork shows

The Hauler is a semi truck, the part of an 18-wheeler that pulls the trailer. JoBuilt is the GTA universe’s heavy-equipment brand. The port of Vice City and the roads heading up to northern Leonida are this kind of vehicle’s natural turf. The official screenshots show it hitched up, ready to hit the road again.

Quick facts

The JoBuilt Hauler is in the catalog’s Service and emergency category. The vehicle appears in media released by Rockstar, but its name hasn’t been announced: the one shown here is a match based on the series or the real model. The screenshots released by Rockstar show it in a scene set in Leonida. Its real-world model is American, which fits the vibe of a Florida-inspired state.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle: Freightliner and Kenworth semi trucks. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on the models it starts from.

The series’ service vehicles belong to the city: police, emergency services, public transit, maintenance. You don’t buy them, you come across them, and sometimes you borrow them.
