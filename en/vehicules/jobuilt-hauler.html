What the artwork shows

The Hauler is a semi truck, the part of an 18-wheeler that pulls the trailer. JoBuilt is the GTA universe’s heavy-equipment brand. The port of Vice City and the roads heading up to northern Leonida are this kind of vehicle’s natural turf. The official screenshots show it hitched up, ready to hit the road again.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle: Freightliner and Kenworth semi trucks. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on the models it starts from.
