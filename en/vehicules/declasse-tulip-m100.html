The essentials

A tuned version of the Tulip, in the spirit of neighborhood muscle cars: swapped engine, reworked suspension, and a look that doesn’t apologize. The M100 suffix nods to the chassis codes tuners use to name their base cars. Trailer 1 has it parked by a suburban house.

Benchmarks

Muscle cars category: that’s where the Declasse Tulip M100 sits in our database. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. It appears in Trailer 1 from December 2023, the game’s very first public reveal. The inspiration is American: the series has always drawn on Detroit automakers and their spin-offs.

What’s still missing (price, performance, where to buy it, what you can modify) will come from data recorded in the game, not estimates.

The model that inspires it

The match chosen for this vehicle is the fourth-generation Chevrolet Malibu. Rockstar never confirms its inspirations: this is an observation based on the official images, not a license.

What this kind of vehicle is for

In a GTA, a muscle car is old-school American power: fast in a straight line, playful in the corners, with an engine sound you recognize from far away.
