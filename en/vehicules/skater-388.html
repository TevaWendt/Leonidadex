The essentials

An offshore racing catamaran, its hull split by a central tunnel that lifts the boat at high speed. These machines top two hundred kilometers per hour and have no use beyond racing and showing off. In-game name not announced.

The model that inspires it

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Skater 388. Rockstar never confirms its inspirations: this is an observation based on the official images, not a license.
