What you need to know

The Slamvan is a pickup slammed to the ground, somewhere between a custom build and a show vehicle. Lowering trucks is a California and Texas tradition, born in the ’80s around Japanese mini-trucks. The version listed here is tied to Trailer 1, with no official name announced.

The real-world model

The match we picked for this vehicle is the 1953-1956 Ford F-Series built as a rat rod. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on its starting models.
