Overview

The Gauntlet Hellfire borrows the look of the Dodge Challenger SRT Hellcat, a car whose spec sheet is pure provocation: a supercharged V8 with more than seven hundred horsepower in a body designed in 1970, with no pretense of finesse. It became a symbol of America’s refusal to tone things down. Trailer 1 lets it loose on a straightaway, hood shaking.

Where its design comes from

The match we picked for this vehicle is the 2018 Dodge Challenger Demon. The studio stays quiet about its references. This match is based on comparing the official images, nothing more.
