The essentials

A Coast Guard response boat with a rigid hull, an inflatable collar and outboard motors. The Response Boat Small is the US Coast Guard’s standard patrol unit. In-game name not announced.

The model that inspires it

The match we picked for this vehicle is the RB-S Coast Guard boat. Rockstar never confirms its inspirations: this is an observation based on the official images, not a license.
