What you need to know

The Deviant’s name comes from Rockstar, through the Ultimate Edition reveal, and our official screenshot shows it decked out in the colors of the American flag, with the cursive monogram on the rear pillar and the interior visible through the window. The AMC Javelin whose proportions it borrows was the market’s underdog: built by an independent automaker much smaller than Detroit’s Big Three, it still won the Trans-Am championship against the Mustangs and Camaros.

The real-world model

The match we picked for this vehicle is the 1972 AMC Javelin. It’s a visual comparison, not official information: the studio doesn’t comment on its inspirations.
