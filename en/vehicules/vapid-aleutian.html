Overview

A big American SUV with three rows of seats, the Aleutian belongs to the class of vehicles built to carry seven people and their luggage on long highway trips. Trailer 2 puts it in a residential driveway, tailgate up.

Where its design comes from

The match chosen for this vehicle is the fourth-generation Ford Expedition. Nobody at Rockstar has confirmed anything. The identification comes from comparing it with the published images.
