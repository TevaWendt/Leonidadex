What the artwork shows

The Speedo is a compact cab-forward van, in the spirit of city work vans. Its small size makes it the go-to vehicle for downtown deliveries, where a Boxville can’t get through. You can see it drive by in Trailer 1.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Chevrolet Express, 2003 onward. It’s a visual comparison, not official information: the studio doesn’t comment on its inspirations.
