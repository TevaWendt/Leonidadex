What the artwork shows

Endurex’s competition road bike, designed after a Pinarello Dogma: carbon frame with asymmetric stays, drop handlebars, deep-section rims. It’s the fastest non-motorized two-wheeler in the game. The Extended Look catches it with a rider going all out.

For context

The Endurex Race Bike is in the catalog’s Misc. category. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. It shows up in the Extended Look, the game’s detailed showcase.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Pinarello Dogma. The studio uses no license and names no reference. This match is a deduction drawn from the images.
