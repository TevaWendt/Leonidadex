What you need to know

A full-size pickup based on the tuned Toyota Tundra, with touches of the Ford F-150 Raptor: the lifted recreational truck kind, built for trails more than for the city. The official screenshots show it perched on its knobby tires.

The real-world model

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Ford F-150 Raptor and a tuned Toyota Tundra. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on its starting models.
