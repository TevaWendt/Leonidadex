What you need to know

The Swift is a private transport helicopter, in the line of the AgustaWestland AW109: sleek profile, retractable landing gear, well-finished cabin. It’s the aircraft for anyone who wants to skip the traffic jams between the business district and the marina. Official screenshots show it sitting on a rooftop helipad.

What the page says

Buckingham Swift is a vehicle in the helicopters category listed on Leonidakit. Officially previewed, named by deduction: this page uses the most likely name and will change it as soon as Rockstar publishes one. It’s visible in one or more screenshots from the official gallery on rockstargames.com. A model of European origin, like most of the series’ luxury sedans and supercars.

The numbers arrive on November 19, 2026: price, top speed, acceleration, capacity and where it’s sold, measured in the game.

The real-world model

The match chosen for this vehicle is AgustaWestland AW109, Bell 222. The studio holds no license and names no references. This match is a deduction drawn from the images.
