What the artwork shows

Emperor is the upscale Japanese brand of the GTA universe, and the Vectre is its compact sports coupe. The name sounds like a shortened “vector”, the kind of aerodynamic-sounding name automakers loved in the 90s. Trailer 2 slips it into a shot of city traffic.

For context

The Emperor Vectre belongs to the sports cars category of the catalog. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. You see it in Trailer 2, across shots of road, sea or city. Its real-world model is Japanese: the series happily borrows sports cars and sedans from Japan.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Lexus RC F. No license is involved here. The studio doesn’t confirm its sources, and this match comes from looking at the published artwork.

In a GTA, the sports category brings together the fast coupes and convertibles you come across in upscale neighborhoods, easy to borrow and fun to drive on coastal roads.
