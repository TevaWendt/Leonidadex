What the artwork shows

Jacksheepe’s tracked excavator, a mix of a Liebherr R954B HD-S and a Samsung SE280LC-2. Swiveling turret, articulated arm, a bucket wide enough to flip a sedan without breaking a sweat. The Extended Look puts it to work on an earthmoving job.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle: Liebherr R954B HD-S, Samsung SE280LC-2. No license is involved here. The studio doesn’t confirm its sources, and this match comes from looking at the released images.
