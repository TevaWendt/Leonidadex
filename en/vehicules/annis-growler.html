Overview

The Growler is the Pfister coupe our database matches to the Porsche 718 Cayman. It shows up in Trailer 2. Its identification is still community-based: the official media doesn’t give it a name.

Where its design comes from

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Porsche 718 Cayman. Rockstar designs replicas, never licensed models, and doesn’t say which real models they’re based on. This match is still just an observation.
