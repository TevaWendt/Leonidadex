Overview

The Growler is the Pfister coupe our database matches to the Porsche 718 Cayman. It shows up in Trailer 2. Its identification is still community-based: the official media doesn’t give it a name.

In a nutshell

On Leonidakit, the Pfister Growler is listed among the sports cars. Officially sighted, name deduced: this page keeps the most likely name and will change it as soon as Rockstar publishes one. Trailer 2 shows it in a scene from the story or from everyday life in Leonida. The inspiration is European: German, Italian and British carmakers inspire the high-end models.

Price, speed, acceleration, where it’s sold and customization options will be added to this page after release, from data recorded in the game.

Where its design comes from

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Porsche 718 Cayman. Rockstar designs replicas, never licensed models, and doesn’t say which real models they’re based on. This match is still just an observation.
