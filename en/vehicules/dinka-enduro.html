What the artwork shows

The Enduro gets its name from Rockstar’s Ultimate Edition reveal, and our official screenshot gives a clean side view: olive green tank with the Dinka logo, worn black seat, high fender, knobby tires, raised exhaust. It’s an 80s Japanese dual-sport bike, the kind that worked as well on the road as on dirt trails. Another screenshot shows it parked in front of Jason’s safehouse, which suggests it’s his.

What the page says

The Dinka Enduro belongs to the catalog’s two-wheelers and quads category. Its name comes straight from Rockstar, which makes it a sure thing in the catalog: no match, no guesswork. Its presence comes from the Ultimate Edition page, where Rockstar details what’s included. The inspiration comes from Japan, a common origin for the series’ compact and sporty models. It’s tied to the game’s Ultimate Edition. A character in the game uses it in official media.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Honda XL 350 and XR 500. It’s a visual comparison, not official information: the studio doesn’t comment on its inspirations.
