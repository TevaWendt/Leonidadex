The essentials

The little tow tractor that pulls baggage carts between the terminal and the plane’s hold. Its shape comes from the Clark CT30, an airport machine nobody ever notices but that drives all over the tarmac. The Extended Look catches it at work at Leonida’s airport.

The model that inspires it

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Clark CT30. Rockstar never confirms its inspirations: this is an observation based on the official images, not a license.
