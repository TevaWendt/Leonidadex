What the artwork shows

A midsize pickup, the format that has won back market share in the United States against full-size trucks that have grown huge. This generation of the Ranger was sold as the sensible middle ground: enough bed to work with, compact enough to park in the city.

Quick facts

The Vapid Reclaimer belongs to the pickups and off-roaders category of the catalog. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. You spot it in Trailer 1, among the shots of the city and the coast. Its real-world model is American, which fits the vibe of a Florida-inspired state.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle is the 2019-2023 Ford Ranger T6. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on its starting models.
