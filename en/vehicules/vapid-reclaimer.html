What the artwork shows

A midsize pickup, the format that has won back market share in the United States against full-size trucks that have grown huge. This generation of the Ranger was sold as the sensible middle ground: enough bed to work with, compact enough to park in the city.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle is the 2019-2023 Ford Ranger T6. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on its starting models.
