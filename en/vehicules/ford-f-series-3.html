Overview

A Ford pickup from the 50s, third generation, the one with wide grilles and rounded fenders. It’s the shape people associate with the farms and gas stations of that era, and a base custom builders really go after. In-game name not announced.

At a glance

On Leonidakit, the F-Series ’50 is listed among the pickups and off-roaders. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. It can be seen in one or more screenshots from the official gallery on rockstargames.com. Its real-world model is American, which fits the vibe of a Florida-inspired state.

Price, speed, acceleration, where it’s sold and customization options will be added to this page after release, from data recorded in the game.

Where its design comes from

The match chosen for this vehicle is the third-generation Ford F-Series. Rockstar never confirms its inspirations: this is an observation based on the official images, not a license.
