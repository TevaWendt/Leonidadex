What you need to know

The Furia is modeled on the Ferrari SF90 Stradale, Ferrari’s first plug-in hybrid road car. The layout is unusual: a turbo V8 in the back and three electric motors, two of them on the front axle, for a total of over a thousand horsepower. It’s a car that can glide along in silence before it transforms. Trailer 1 sends it flying at full speed down an open stretch.

For context

The Grotti Furia is a vehicle in the Supercars category, listed on Leonidakit. The vehicle appears in media released by Rockstar, but its name hasn’t been announced: the one shown here is a match with the series or the real-world model. Its first appearance goes back to Trailer 1, the one that revealed Leonida and Vice City. The inspiration is European: German, Italian and British carmakers inspire the high-end models.

The numbers arrive on November 19, 2026: price, top speed, acceleration, capacity and where it’s sold, all measured in the game.

The real-world model

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Ferrari 488 Pista and SF90 Stradale. The studio holds no license and names no reference. This match is a deduction drawn from the images.
