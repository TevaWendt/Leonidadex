What the artwork shows

Progen’s track supercar, traced from the McLaren Senna: a huge wing, scoops everywhere, windows cut all the way down into the lower doors. Everything is sacrificed for downforce, starting with comfort. The Extended Look shows it parked in a garage.

For context

The Progen Emerus is in the catalog’s supercars category. Officially sighted, name deduced: this page keeps the most likely name and will change it as soon as Rockstar publishes one. It appears in An Extended Look, the long showcase video released by Rockstar. The inspiration is European: German, Italian and British carmakers inspire the high-end models.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle is the McLaren Senna. It’s a visual comparison, not official information: the studio doesn’t comment on its inspirations.
