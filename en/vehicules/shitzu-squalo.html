What you need to know

The Squalo’s name is spelled out in the Ultimate Edition presentation, and the official screenshots leave no doubt: the Shitzu logo and the Squalo name are readable on the hull and the windshield. You can see the palm-leaf livery, the teak deck, the fishing rods, a scuba tank and the registration number VB 0522 05. The hull is that of the Chris-Craft Stinger 390x, an 80s sport speedboat whose low profile and pointed nose are inseparable from the image of Miami.

The real-world model

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Chris-Craft Stinger 390x. No license is involved here. The studio doesn’t confirm its sources, and this match comes from looking at the published images.
