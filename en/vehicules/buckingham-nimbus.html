The essentials

A twin-engine business jet modeled on the Cessna Citation X, for a long time the fastest civilian plane on the market. Slim fuselage, sharply swept wings, engines mounted at the tail. Trailer 1 shows it crossing the Leonida sky at high altitude.

Benchmarks

Planes category: that’s where the Buckingham Nimbus sits in our database. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. It appears in Trailer 1 from December 2023, the game’s very first public showing. It’s an American model, like a good share of the vehicles in Leonida.

What’s still missing (price, performance, where to buy it, what you can modify) will come from data recorded in the game, not estimates.

The model that inspires it

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Cessna Citation X. Rockstar never confirms its inspirations: this is an observation based on the official images, not a license.
