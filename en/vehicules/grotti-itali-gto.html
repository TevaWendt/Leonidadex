Overview

The Itali GTO borrows the design language of the Ferrari 812 Superfast: front-mounted V12, compact tail, clustered exhaust tips. The 812 is one of the last Ferraris with a naturally aspirated twelve-cylinder engine, which gives it a special status among collectors today. Whether it comes to Leonida is still to be confirmed.

Where its design comes from

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Ferrari 812 Superfast. The studio stays quiet about its references. This match is based on comparing the official images, nothing more.
