What the artwork shows

The official screenshots show a turquoise muscle car with gold stripes, a trunk spoiler and three-spoke wheels, with the Sirius name spelled out on the rear fender. Neither our previous list nor the ones on other sites mentioned this model: it’s a find that comes straight from Rockstar’s material. The silhouette is that of a 1970 Mercury Cougar, the Mustang’s pricier cousin. The manufacturer hasn’t been announced.

The real-world match

The match chosen for this vehicle is the 1970 Mercury Cougar. The studio doesn’t use any license and doesn’t name any reference. This match is a deduction drawn from the images.
