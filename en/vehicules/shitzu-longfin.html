What the artwork shows

A big-game fishing boat with a tall cabin, an arch and outriggers. The kind of boat you rent by the day all along the Florida coast, and that also gets used, in fiction as in real life, for other kinds of business.

Quick facts

The Shitzu Longfin belongs to the Boats and jet skis category of the catalog. Reported by the community, not confirmed by our own checks: the page exists so nothing gets lost, with a status that says so clearly.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match chosen for this vehicle is an offshore sportfishing boat. It’s a visual comparison, not official information: the studio doesn’t comment on its inspirations.
