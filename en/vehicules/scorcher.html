What you need to know

A mountain bike with suspension, the Scorcher is made for trails and parks rather than the road. Mount Kalaga National Park, listed on our map, is exactly the kind of place where this type of bike comes into its own. The official screenshots show it leaning against a low wall.

The real-world model

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Saracen Zen, Rockshox Domain 180mm. It’s a visual comparison, not official information: the studio doesn’t comment on its inspirations.
