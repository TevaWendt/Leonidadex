What you need to know

The Crown Victoria was the standard American police car for more than twenty years. The Stanier LE takes on the same role: rear-wheel-drive sedan, body-on-frame chassis, roof light bar.

The real-world model

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Ford Crown Victoria Police Interceptor. Rockstar never confirms its inspirations: this is an observation based on the official images, not a license.
