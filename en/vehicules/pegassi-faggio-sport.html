What you need to know

The modern version of the Pegassi scooter, with the angular look of today’s little 50 cc scooters: plastic fairing, solid wheels, headlight built into the front shield. Far from the retro charm of the classic Faggio, it’s the ride of delivery workers and high schoolers, the one you see everywhere on the streets of Vice City in the Extended Look. The word Sport promises nothing: it won’t outrun the line of cars it’s weaving past.

The real-world model

The match chosen for this vehicle is Piaggio Zip, Peugeot V-Clic and Kymco Agility 50. The studio doesn’t use any license and doesn’t name any reference. This match is a deduction drawn from the images.
