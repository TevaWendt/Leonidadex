Overview

A mid-size box truck, bigger than the Mule and easier to handle than a semi. It’s traditionally used for heists that need cargo space.

Where its design comes from

The match chosen for this vehicle is the International 4700 Delivery Truck Chassis. Rockstar designs replicas, never licensed models, and doesn’t say what they’re based on. This match is still just an observation.
