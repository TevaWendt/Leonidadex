What you need to know

The Sabre Turbo blends the Chevrolet Chevelle and the Oldsmobile Cutlass, two General Motors midsize cars that shared the same platform in the early 70s. It was the golden age of big V8s, brutally cut short by the 1973 oil crisis. As for the Turbo suffix, it’s a tuning thing: these engines never had forced induction from the factory.

What the page says

The Declasse Sabre Turbo is a vehicle in the muscle cars category, listed on Leonidakit. Officially sighted, name deduced: this page keeps the most likely name and will change it as soon as Rockstar publishes one. It appears in Trailer 1 from December 2023, the game’s very first public reveal. Its real-world model is American, which fits the vibe of a Florida-inspired state.

The numbers arrive on November 19, 2026: price, top speed, acceleration, capacity and where it’s sold, all measured in the game.

The real-world model

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Chevrolet Chevelle and Oldsmobile Cutlass. Take it for what it is: a reading of the released images. Rockstar has never commented on the models it starts from.
