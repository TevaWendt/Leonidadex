Overview

The performance version of the Baller: redesigned bumper, quad exhaust tips, big-diameter wheels. The fast luxury SUV is an archetype that feels right at home in Vice City’s wealthy neighborhoods.

Where its design comes from

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Range Rover L460. Nobody at Rockstar has confirmed anything. The identification comes from comparing it with the published images.
