Overview

The Journey II is a Class A motorhome, modeled on the Winnebago Chieftain. The brand name, Zirconium, plays on imitation: zircon is the stone that looks like a diamond without being one. For a budget vacation vehicle, that’s a pretty good joke. These rigs are part of the scenery on Florida roads, especially in winter.

In a nutshell

On Leonidakit, the Zirconium Journey II is listed among the vans and cargo vans. Reported by the community, not confirmed by our own checks: the page exists so nothing gets lost, with a status that says so clearly. The inspiration is American: the series has always drawn on Detroit automakers and their spin-offs.

Price, speed, acceleration, where it’s sold and customization options will be added to this page after release, from data recorded in the game.

Where its design comes from

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Winnebago Chieftain. The studio stays quiet about its references. This match is based on comparing the official images, nothing more.
