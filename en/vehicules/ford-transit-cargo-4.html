What you need to know

The panel version of the Transit, with no side windows in the back. It’s the setup delivery drivers and tradespeople use, the one that gives you the most cargo space and the least view of what you’re hauling. In-game name not announced.

For context

The Transit Cargo is a vehicle in the Vans and cargo vans category, listed on Leonidakit. Officially sighted, named by deduction: this page keeps the most likely name and will change it as soon as Rockstar publishes one. It appears in Trailer 1 from December 2023, the game’s very first public showing. Its real-world model is American, which fits the vibe of a Florida-inspired state.

The numbers arrive on November 19, 2026: price, top speed, acceleration, capacity and where it’s sold, all measured in the game.

The real-world model

The match chosen for this vehicle is the Ford Transit Cargo. No license is involved here. The studio doesn’t confirm its sources, and this match comes from looking at the published images.
