What the images show

Chopper with a heavily raked fork, high handlebars and a skinny front wheel. A style that comes straight from American motorcycle clubs, and one that feels right at home on long, straight coastal roads.

Road notes

LCC Avarus belongs to the two-wheelers and quads category of the catalog. Officially previewed, named by deduction: this page uses the most likely name and will change it as soon as Rockstar publishes one. It appears in the second trailer from May 2026, the one that follows Jason and Lucia across the state.

This page will fill in once the game is out: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match chosen for this vehicle is Generica mostly-naked chopper. This is a visual comparison, not official information: the studio doesn’t comment on its inspirations.

Its place in a GTA game

The series’ two-wheelers squeeze through where cars get stuck: heavy traffic, alleys, narrow paths. They’re fast, but they don’t forgive crashes.
