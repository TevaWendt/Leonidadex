The essentials

A tuned 1970 Dodge Challenger, one of the most sought-after muscle cars today. The 1970 version is the one whose design everyone agrees on, with its quad-headlight grille and straight shoulder line. The Custom suffix means a reworked engine and chassis. Not confirmed by Rockstar.

The model that inspires it

The match we picked for this vehicle is the 1970 Dodge Challenger. Nobody at Rockstar has confirmed anything. The identification comes from comparing it with the released images.
