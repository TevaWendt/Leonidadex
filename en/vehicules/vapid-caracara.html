What the artwork shows

The base version of the Caracara, before the beefed-up 4x4 variant. A compact short-cab pickup, made more for dirt trails than for the highway.

What the page says

The Vapid Caracara belongs to the pickups and off-roaders category of the catalog. We’ve seen it in the official images, with no label. The name used here is temporary and will change once the studio first mentions it. You spot it in Trailer 1, among the shots of the city and the coast. Its real-world model is American, which fits the vibe of a Florida-inspired state.

This page will fill in with the game: purchase price, measured performance, dealerships where you can find it, options at Rideout Customs.

The real-world match

The match we picked for this vehicle is the Ford F-150, Hennessey VelociRaptor 6X6. No license is involved here. The studio doesn’t confirm its sources, and this match comes from studying the released images.
