Verification and sources

Every line in the lists and every claim on this page has a status and links to a page opened on the date shown. Rockstar pages are cited with the last time they were opened directly; their texts were reread on September 27, 2026 in the sources that reproduce them.

Official

Named or described by Rockstar: a page on the official site, Newswire, a video, or official text reproduced by the press.

Seen in media

Visible in a trailer, an official screenshot or the Extended Look. It exists on screen; we don’t know the price or whether you can buy it.

Community identification

A match made by players (wiki, gtadb.org), cited as such, never presented as confirmed.

Series benchmark

What GTA V, GTA Online, GTA IV or San Andreas do. A price from the series stays in its column: it doesn’t apply to GTA VI.

To be confirmed

Nothing published by Rockstar. The line is there to say what we expect, not to claim it.
