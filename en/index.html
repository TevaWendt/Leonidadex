The State of Leonida

Six regions presented by Rockstar. You’ll find the six counties and how certain each one is on the map.

Region

Vice City

The capital of sun and excess, far from the ’80s but as hungry as ever.

Vice City brings together beaches, shopping districts, a cruise port and Downtown’s skyscrapers. Rockstar mentions Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka and VC Port, and makes it the densest, liveliest area in Leonida.

Region

Leonida Keys

A string of islands where people live off the sea, fishing and quiet smuggling.

A tropical archipelago linked by long roads over the water. Locals live off fishing, seaside bars and quiet smuggling: Jason lives there on a property owned by Brian Heder, whose boatyard still moves goods on the side.

Open the page
Region

Grassrivers

Leonida’s wild swamps, where the alligator is the most famous attraction.

The country’s largest subtropical wilderness: mangroves, slow channels and flooded plains you cross by airboat. Rockstar calls it Leonida’s untamed jewel, as beautiful as it is dangerous.

Region

Port Gellhorn

A faded beach resort of cheap motels and closed-down attractions.

Once a vacation destination, Port Gellhorn now lives off what’s left: cheap motels, empty malls and closed-down attractions. A place to lie low and take the jobs nobody else wants.

Region

Ambrosia

The state’s industrial heartland, where American industry and old-school values still rule.

Leonida’s farming and industrial side, built around the Allied Crystal sugar refinery. It provides the jobs; a local biker gang provides, according to Rockstar, pretty much everything else.

Region

Mount Kalaga National Park

Fresh air on the northern border: hunting, fishing and off-road trails.

Northern Leonida’s national park: forests, hills, rivers and muddy trails. Rockstar presents it as a place for hunting, fishing and off-roading, far from the cities.

Frequently asked questions

Short, sourced answers, no rumors.

When does GTA VI come out?
On November 19, 2026. Rockstar confirmed the date after two delays in a row, first from fall 2025, then from May 26, 2026.
Which platforms?
PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch. No PC version has been announced so far.
Is there an online mode at launch?
Rockstar hasn’t announced any online mode for release. The game is presented as a single-player experience centered on Jason and Lucia’s story.
What’s the difference between the editions?
The Standard Edition includes the game. The Ultimate Edition adds a selection of vehicles, weapons and outfits spread throughout the story. Pre-ordering before November 20 also gets you the Vintage Vice City Pack.
Is this site official?
No. Leonidakit is an independent project made by a player, with no ties to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
Where is GTA VI set?
In the State of Leonida, inspired by Florida, with Vice City as its main city. Rockstar has presented six regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Each one has its own page on this site and its own area on the map.
How many playable characters?
Two: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. It’s the first time a female protagonist is playable in a mainline GTA. The other characters presented by Rockstar (Cal, Boobie, Dre’Quan, Real Dimez, Raul, Brian) revolve around them.
How much does GTA VI cost?
Since pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, store listings have shown the Standard Edition at €79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at €99.99. Prices can vary from one store to another: check yours. In the game itself, no prices have been published (cars, weapons, houses).
Where does the site’s information come from?
From material published by Rockstar: the two trailers, the Extended Look, the screenshot gallery, the character and region pages, and the edition artwork. Each page states its level of certainty. Nothing comes from leaks.
What happens on the site on release day?
The calculator works right now with your own values. In-game prices, rewards and durations will be added once they’re documented, with their sources. The map, the pages and local tracking stay available.
