The essentials

The map of Leonida has 3,291 places: the 47 regions, cities, neighborhoods and counties have their own page (see the neighborhoods, cities and counties), and the other 3,244 have theirs here: 203 transport places, 2,823 buildings, 113 notable places, 103 natural areas, 2 safehouses. 857 have a known in-game name, 2,387 an official screenshot. Each one has its page, linked to its neighborhood, city or county and to its neighbors; the 2,240 pages with at least two real facts (neighborhood or county, position, real-world equivalent, screenshot) are offered to search engines; the others (numbered buildings, uncertain names) exist and are waiting for more information.
