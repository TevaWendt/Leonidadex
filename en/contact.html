CONTACT US

Every fix
starts with a detail.

A page, a calculation, an answer from Léo or an image that needs another look? Write it here: the message goes to the publisher’s inbox, and the site keeps none of it.

The site’s email address

contact [at] leonidakit [dot] com

Use the form

No reply time is guaranteed. For a question about your data, choose the reason “Question about my data”.

01

Before you write

What happens when you send

Three steps, and the site keeps nothing.

01

You write

The form keeps a draft in your browser until you send it. Nothing goes out until you click “Send message”.

02

The site passes it on

A small site function, hosted by Vercel, checks the message and then hands it to Brevo, the email delivery service. It doesn’t store it anywhere.

03

The publisher gets it

The message lands in the publisher’s inbox with a reference number, which you also see on screen. If you leave your address, the reply goes there.

In short No reply time is guaranteed. Don’t send passwords, ID documents, bank details or information about someone else.

02

To save time

The most useful details

A good report comes down to four things: the page, what’s wrong, the fix, a source.

  • Fix a page

    Add its URL, the info you think is wrong, your suggested fix and a source that can be checked.

  • Suggest content

    Give an exact name and its context. An official source is best; say clearly if it’s a community identification.

    The site’s statuses ↗

  • Report an image

    Give the page, the image in question, the reason and the expected source or credit. Don’t send personal documents.
