Overview

The Jack of Hearts is Boobie Ike’s strip club, at the heart of his Vice City empire. Rockstar mentions it in the character’s introduction: the club’s income funds the Only Raw Records label, and Dre’Quan books artists there. The official screenshots show Boobie in front of the red neon sign, on a street at night. The club is marked on the Vice City map based on these screenshots.
