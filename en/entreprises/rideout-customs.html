Rideout Customs

The shop where Leonida’s cars get a whole new look.

The car customization shop presented with the Ultimate Edition: custom paint, rims and interiors, with a gold Manana on oversized rims.

Overview

Rideout Customs is the car customization shop that Rockstar shows in the Ultimate Edition artwork. The official screenshots show an Albany Manana with gold flake paint and gold rims, its red interior and a mechanic under a yellow car. The edition text promises detailed interiors, exquisite rims and donk styling, and says the shop only opens with that edition. The shop is marked on the Vice City map based on these screenshots. Prices and options will be added when the game comes out.

What the artwork shows

Rideout Customs is the customization shop shown with the Ultimate Edition. The official screenshots show a gold Albany Manana on oversized rims, its red interior and a yellow car being worked on, a mechanic underneath. It’s the shop that signals the return of in-depth vehicle customization.

In the game

In the game, this is where paint, wheels, interiors and finishes should get installed, with a price and a required level for each option: that’s what the vehicle pages plan for in their Customization card. The shop is marked on the Vice City map; its prices will be recorded at release.

Key points

  • Customization shop shown in the Ultimate Edition artwork.
  • Detailed interiors, exquisite rims and donk styling, according to the edition text.
  • Marked on the map thanks to the official screenshots.
