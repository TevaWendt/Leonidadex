      01
      The rules
      How to play
      1. Five clues a day
        The draw changes at midnight, Paris time. Everyone gets the same clues: you can compare your score with your friends.
      2. One station per clue
        A host, their on-air name, the station’s style or a song heard in its preview: pick the station it goes with.
      3. The answer, right away
        The right station lights up on the dial, with its style, its hosts, the clue’s status, its source and the link to its page.
      4. Your streak, then free play
        Finish the daily game to extend your streak and earn your points. Then play as much as you like, at random.

      03
      Questions
      Frequently asked questions
      Do the stations in Guess the Station come from leaks?
      No. Only the six stations Rockstar presented on October 8, 2026, and the songs Game Informer noted in their official previews. The site uses nothing from the leaks.
      Are there other stations in GTA VI?
      Rockstar hasn’t said how many there will be in total. When a new station is presented, it joins the game.
      Is my score saved anywhere?
      Only on your device, in your browser’s storage. Nothing is sent; sharing only passes the text of your result to the app you choose.
      Can you listen to the stations?
      Rockstar offers a preview of each station on the Music page of its site. Each station’s page links to it.
