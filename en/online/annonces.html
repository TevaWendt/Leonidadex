Announcements, one by one

What Rockstar, Take-Two and the stores have said about the online game in 2026, in order, with the source. No announcement of a GTA Online for GTA VI yet.

  • 9announcements
  • 6official
  • 18sources

2026 timeline

The statements, in order

  1. 01February 3, 2026

    Take-Two, quarterly earnings

    “Every reason to believe” GTA Online will continue

    Strauss Zelnick says he has every reason to believe Take-Two will keep supporting GTA Online, backed by a loyal community. He cites NBA 2K Online: its second version came out in China in 2017 without the first one shutting down, and both are still running.

    Official12

  2. 02May 21, 2026

    Take-Two, full-year earnings

    GTA Online will stay on the market

    It’s hard to predict how GTA Online will do after GTA VI, says Strauss Zelnick, but the game will stay on the market, with lots of loyal players. GTA Online grew 6% over the fiscal year.

    Official1

  3. 03June 24, 2026

    Rockstar Games, pre-orders

    “A single-player experience”

    The pre-order press release presents GTA VI as a single-player experience, out November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, without a word about GTA Online. Digital pre-orders give you a month of GTA+, which is used in today’s GTA Online.

    Official123

  4. 04June 24, 2026

    PlayStation and Xbox

    The stores say “single-player”

    The PlayStation Blog promises a single-player experience; the FAQ on the PlayStation page answers “single-player experience” to the multiplayer question, and the Xbox page lists only single-player.

    Official123

  5. 05August 7, 2026

    Take-Two, quarterly earnings

    A steady pace of updates

    Strauss Zelnick: the pace of GTA Online updates hasn’t really changed, Take-Two is putting significant resources into it and plans to keep supporting the game.

    Official1

  6. 06August 2026

    Jason Schreier, Bloomberg

    Nothing on the online game anytime soon

    According to the journalist, Take-Two only talks about GTA VI’s single-player side, and Rockstar won’t talk about the online game for a long time.

    Community identification1

  7. 07September 17, 2026

    Take-Two, shareholder meeting

    GTA Online “after GTA 6’s release and beyond”

    Take-Two repeats that GTA VI is a single-player game, coming out in November, and that GTA Online will be supported after its release and beyond. Strauss Zelnick adds that no recurring spending is expected for this launch.

    Official123

  8. 08September 2026

    Dan Clancy, Twitch’s boss

    Multiplayer “next year”?

    Speaking to Bloomberg, Twitch’s boss says he has talked a lot with Rockstar and expects a viewership peak with GTA VI, then a bigger one with multiplayer, next year. Rockstar and Take-Two haven’t confirmed anything.

    Community identification12

  9. 09October 1, 2026

    ESRB rating

    M, with in-game purchases

    The official site shows the ESRB M rating (ages 17 and up), with in-game purchases listed. Tech Times reads it as no online game at launch; neither Rockstar nor the ESRB has said so.

    Seen in media12
