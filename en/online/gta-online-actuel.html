Today’s GTA Online, the one from GTA V

GTA V’s online game, launched in 2013, keeps going. It’s a benchmark for picturing GTA VI’s online game: a ballpark, never a GTA VI number.

01 / 05 Chapter

Its beginnings

  1. 01

    GTA V comes out on September 17, 2013 on PS3 and Xbox 360: over $800 million in sales on day one, according to Take-Two.

    Official1

  2. 02

    GTA Online opens on October 1, 2013, two weeks after the story, included with the game.

    Community identification1

  3. 03

    A session then brings together up to 16 players: co-op missions, deathmatches, races and free roam.

    Community identification1

02 / 05 Chapter

What you can buy in it

  1. 01

    Shark Cards add GTA$ to the online game’s balance in exchange for real money; you can buy them in the game or in the stores.

    Official1

  2. 02

    From June 18 to July 22, 2026, the biggest Shark Cards gave 40% more GTA$.

    Official1

  3. 03

    GTA+, a subscription launched on March 29, 2022 on PS5 and Xbox Series, cost $5.99 per month, with GTA$500,000 every month; it went up to $7.99 in 2024.

    Community identification12

  4. 04

    Since November 6, 2025, GTA+ includes access to GTA Online on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, where the online game is also sold on its own.

    Community identification12

03 / 05 Chapter

Its latest updates

  1. 01

    GTA V

    A Safehouse in the Hills

    December 10, 2025: the return of Michael De Santa, mansions in Vinewood Hills, a new mission creator and vehicles.

    Community identification1

  2. 02

    GTA V

    The Kortz Center Heist

    July 14, 2026: an art heist at the Kortz Center, solo or with up to four players, for anyone who owns a mansion and its Art Studio add-on.

    Official1

  3. 03

    To go with it, GTA Online was playable without PS Plus or Game Pass from July 14 to 20, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series.

    Community identification1

04 / 05 Chapter

The free month of GTA+ with GTA VI

  1. 01

    A digital pre-order of GTA VI made before November 20, 2026 gives you a month of GTA+. It’s activated in the console store and works in this GTA Online, not in GTA VI.

    Official12

  2. 02

    Activate it no later than March 31, 2027 on PS5, or within 180 days on Xbox; once per account; it then renews at the regular price unless you cancel it.

    Official12

  3. 03

    On PlayStation, the offer isn’t valid in Bahrain, China, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Qatar, Russia or Taiwan.

    Official1

05 / 05 Chapter

And after GTA VI?

  1. 01

    Take-Two promises to keep GTA Online going after GTA VI’s release, “and beyond”, with the same pace of updates.

    Official12

  2. 02

    No transfer of characters, money or assets to GTA VI has been announced. The only precedent: the Enhanced version of GTA V on PC, in March 2025, which let you carry over your progress.

    Community identification12
