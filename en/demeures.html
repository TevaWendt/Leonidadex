The residences of Leonida

These pages bring together the places tied to the characters’ lives: houses, lodgings and other addresses mentioned in their stories.

Find the links to the characters and nearby places. A residence shown or described by Rockstar isn’t, on its own, a home the player will be able to buy. Understanding statuses.

01

Official sources

What Rockstar has shown

Here, a residence is a place where someone lives, as described or shown by Rockstar. Here’s where each piece of info comes from.

  1. May 6, 2025Official

    Official profiles and Trailer 2

    Jason’s profile: he lives for free in a property owned by Brian Heder, in exchange for helping out with debt collecting. Lucia’s: the penitentiary before the story. Cal’s: at home, in front of his scanner. Trailer 2 shows Jason’s stilt house in Key Lento: he’s fixing the roof, Brian shows up asking for “his checks”, a neighbor calls out to him.

  2. June 24, 2026Official

    Editions

    The “Jason’s Safehouse Vehicles” screenshot shows the repaired house, with the Ganado, the Dinka Enduro and the Crest kayak. It’s the only official name for a home in the game. The editions also describe personal garages “throughout Leonida”, with a weapon locker and a stash for stolen goods.

  3. August 27, 2026Seen in media

    Extended Look, then Famitsu

    A pink-decorated apartment where Lucia picks an outfit while Jason worries about being late; a fridge, a TV showing in-game shows. The next day, Rob Nelson (Rockstar North) told Famitsu: safehouses change from chapter to chapter, like the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2; you can cut your hair at home, with limited options; you start with three vehicle slots, which you can expand by buying garages.

A residence that’s been shown isn’t property for sale: none of the four pages has a price, and none will until Rockstar publishes one.

05

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Can you buy a house in GTA VI?
Nothing has been announced. Rob Nelson told Famitsu (August 28, 2026) that safehouses change with the story and that only garages can be bought. There are no home prices; the calculator lets you enter your own.
Where does Jason live?
In the Leonida Keys, in Key Lento, in a stilt house lent to him by Brian Heder. Rockstar calls it “Jason’s Safehouse” in the screenshot released with the editions (June 24, 2026).
Where does Lucia live?
Before the story, in the Leonida Penitentiary. The Extended Look (August 27, 2026) then shows a pink-decorated apartment where the duo gets ready; Rockstar hasn’t said whose it is or where.
How many vehicles can you keep at home?
Three at first, according to Rob Nelson (Famitsu, August 28, 2026). To keep more, you need to buy garages on the map; otherwise, you sell a vehicle to free up a slot.
Can you move to another house?
Yes, but not by buying one: Rob Nelson explains that you move from one safehouse to another as the chapters go by, and that the interior changes a bit with the story.

Sources and statuses

Every claim on this page has a status and comes from a page checked on the date shown. Rockstar pages are cited with the date we last opened them directly; their text was reread on September 27, 2026, in the sources that reproduce it.

Official

A page or video published by Rockstar (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look), or words from a Rockstar lead reported by a named outlet, with the date.

Seen in media

What an official image or clip shows, with no Rockstar text saying so: a trailer shot, a screenshot, a moment from the Extended Look, with the timestamp when known.

Community identification

A match made by players, a wiki or a fan site (real place, actor, county). Cited as such, never presented as confirmed.

To be confirmed

A question with no published answer: price, purchase, access, role in the story. We write it down so we don’t guess.
