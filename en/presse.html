In short

The site in three sentences

Use as is, or cut it down.

Leonidakit is a fan site about GTA VI: an interactive map of Leonida, pages on vehicles, weapons, places and characters, a calculator to plan purchases, budget and play time, and Léo, an assistant that answers questions about the game.

Every piece of information has a status (Official, Seen in media, Community identification, Series benchmark, To be confirmed) and its source; nothing is made up.

Free, no account and no cookies, in 6 languages, run by an individual and independent of both Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive.

Citing the site

Citing information

Three simple rules, so your audience knows where each thing comes from.

An official announcement

Cite Rockstar’s source when the page gives one: it is on the page’s “Source” line, or under “Sources for this file” for a place or a character. Leonidakit only passes it on.

A page of the site

To cite the site, write “Leonidakit” with a link to the page the information comes from: the map, the calculator and every vehicle, weapon or place page have their own address.

A point still to be confirmed

A “Community identification” or a point marked “To be confirmed” is not an announcement: present it as such, with its status.

Example: “Source: Leonidakit, interactive map of Leonida (leonidakit.com/en/carte.html)”.

Credit lines ready to copy

  • In an article

    Source: Leonidakit, interactive map of Leonida (https://www.leonidakit.com/en/carte.html)

  • Under a video

    Data: Leonidakit (https://www.leonidakit.com/en/), unofficial GTA VI fan site

  • In a post

    Seen on leonidakit.com, the unofficial GTA VI fan site

05

What you can reuse

Free to use, with a link

The kit files are made to be reused. The game’s visuals, however, belong to Rockstar Games.

Yes, freely

  • Leonidakit’s logo and icon, on a light or dark background, at whatever size you need.
  • Screenshots of the site, in an article, a video, a thumbnail or a post, crediting Rockstar Games for the game visuals they show.
  • The site’s figures and information, with a link to the page.

Please avoid

  • Changing the logo (colors, proportions) or blending it with another one.
  • Implying a partnership, endorsement or sponsorship that does not exist.
  • Presenting the game’s official visuals as the site’s own: they come from Rockstar Games, who must be credited.
06

Independence

What the site is not

A fan project, not an official site of the game.

Leonidakit is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and GTA VI belong to them.

No content on the site is paid for by anyone. Talking with the press, a creator or a brand never changes a status, a source or a figure.

  • Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two

    No official link to the game’s publisher or its parent company.

  • No paid content

    No sponsored articles or paid rankings: the site doesn’t sell anything.

  • Every fact sourced

    A status and a source on every page, so you can check.
