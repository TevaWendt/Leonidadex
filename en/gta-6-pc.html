GTA VI on PC:
what Rockstar has said, and nothing else.

GTA VI comes out on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has announced no PC version, no date and no Steam page. Here’s what’s official, what the series did before, where the announcement will come from, and why any “PC version” offered today is a scam.

03

Key points

Any GTA VI “PC version” or “beta” offered today is a scam

There is no PC version, no beta and no early access. What you’re being offered isn’t the game.

No PC version, no beta, no key: anything presented that way before a Rockstar announcement is fake.

A file to download, a “beta key” to win, “early access” in exchange for a payment or a sign-up: these are the usual forms fake versions take, and they come back before every big game. The goal is to steal an account or a payment, or to install malware.

  • GTA VI can only be bought on the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Store and from retailers selling the box (a code, not a disc).
  • Rockstar has opened no beta and no public test: nobody can invite you to one.
  • Buying a “key” or an account from a third-party seller goes against Rockstar’s policy, which says so on its account security page.
  • No site, no video, no message can give you the game “early”; in August 2026, fake “Extended Look” and “demo” sites were spreading a program that steals passwords.
  • Rockstar says the same about its accounts: don’t download anything from a third-party site, and watch out for emails that imitate its own.
  • If you entered a login or a payment on one of those pages, change your password and tell your bank.
