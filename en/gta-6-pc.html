03

Key points

Any GTA VI “PC version” or “beta” offered today is a scam

There is no PC version, no beta and no early access. What you’re being offered isn’t the game.

No PC version, no beta, no key: anything presented that way before a Rockstar announcement is fake.

A file to download, a “beta key” to win, “early access” in exchange for a payment or a sign-up: these are the usual forms fake versions take, and they come back before every big game. The goal is to steal an account or a payment, or to install malware.

  • GTA VI can only be bought on the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Store and from retailers selling the box (a code, not a disc).
  • Rockstar has opened no beta and no public test: nobody can invite you to one.
  • Never give out your Rockstar login details: Rockstar Support warns that fake third-party “services” ask for them to steal accounts.
  • No site, no video, no message can give you the game “early”; in August 2026, fake “Extended Look” and “demo” sites were spreading a program that steals passwords.
  • Rockstar says the same about its accounts: don’t download anything from a third-party site, and watch out for emails that imitate its own.
  • If you entered a login or a payment on one of those pages, change your password and tell your bank.

Questions

Questions people ask about GTA 6 on PC

Will GTA 6 come out on PC?
Rockstar hasn’t announced anything: no PC version, no date. The game comes out on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only.
When will GTA 6 come out on PC?
No date exists. For context, GTA V came to PC a year and a half after the consoles, Red Dead Redemption 2 a year after: these are series benchmarks, not an announcement.
Is there a Steam page or system requirements for GTA 6?
No. No page on any PC store, no system requirements: nothing going around about this is official.
Is a GTA 6 beta on PC possible?
No. Rockstar has opened no beta and no public test. A “beta” or a “key” offered today is a scam.
Can you play GTA 6 on PC through streaming?
No, according to Xbox: on October 7, 2026, Matthew Ball, Xbox’s head of strategy, wrote that the game would not be cloud-streamed either exclusively or to PC (remarks reported by Dexerto). Nothing else is announced: GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. What Rockstar hasn’t said yet
