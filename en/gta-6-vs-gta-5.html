02

Word for word

The GTA VI map vs the GTA V map: what Rockstar said

No official figure. What Rockstar North said, as reported on August 27, 2026: by a creator invited to the studio, who quotes Rob Nelson, then by The New York Times, which quotes the studio. The words are quoted as they are, in English.

Rockstar has never published these figures on its website or its Newswire: Rob Nelson, who co-runs Rockstar North, gave them to creators invited to the studio in July 2026, and they were reported on August 27, the day of An Extended Look. Based on the same figures, Vice City alone would be twice the size of Los Santos, GTA V’s city. The New York Times confirmed the comparison with Red Dead Redemption 2 with the studio.

No surface area in square miles or km² exists, for GTA VI or for GTA V: Rockstar has never published one. On the site, the interactive map lists the places spotted in official media, region by region; it doesn’t measure the game’s surface area.

05

Questions

Questions people ask about GTA 6 vs GTA 5

Is the GTA 6 map bigger than the GTA 5 map?
Rockstar hasn’t published any surface area. Based on figures from Rob Nelson of Rockstar North, reported by TGG on August 27, 2026, the map is twice the size of GTA V’s and three times that of Red Dead Redemption 2; The New York Times attributes the comparison with Red Dead Redemption 2’s playable area to the studio. The exact words are on this page.
Is GTA 6 the sequel to GTA 5?
No: it’s a new game, in a new state, Leonida, with two new heroes, Lucia and Jason. Like every GTA, it keeps the spirit of the series without continuing the previous game’s story.
Is GTA 6 set in Vice City like in 2002?
Vice City is indeed Leonida’s big city, but the game is set in the present day, not in the ’80s of GTA: Vice City. The Vice City page
How many playable characters are there in GTA 6?
Two, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, versus three in GTA V (Michael, Franklin, Trevor).
Will GTA 6 be more expensive than GTA 5?
GTA 6 is $79.99 for the Standard and $99.99 for the Ultimate in the United States, prices announced by Take-Two, and €79.99 and €99.99 on European stores; GTA V cost $59.99 on the US PlayStation Store in 2013. Prices and collector’s box
