Dirty South Classics

Southern rap classics.

Dirty South Classics in GTA VI: classic rap and hip-hop from the Southern United States, with Trick Daddy and Trina. Presented on October 8, 2026.

Listen to the official previewThe six stationsThe Leonida radio

Overview

Dirty South Classics plays classic rap and hip-hop from the Southern United States, the “Dirty South”. Its hosts are two big names in rap from Miami, the city that inspires Vice City: Trick Daddy, called Trick on the air, and Trina, under her stage name. The choice fits what Rockstar says about the game’s musical spirit: “We love Miami music, particularly Miami bass”.

On the air

Trick Daddy
On-air name Trick
Trina
On-air name Trina

What the announcement says, and doesn’t say

Rockstar hasn’t published the track list. Southern rap tracks heard in the Extended Look, like “Pop Bottles” by Birdman and Lil Wayne or “Skrilla” by Kodak Black, aren’t tied to any station.

What we know for the game

Like the other stations, it can be heard in a car and, for the first time in the series, on foot: with Jason’s or Lucia’s phone and earbuds, according to Rockstar.

Key points

  • Style: classic rap and hip-hop from the Southern United States.
  • On the air: Trick Daddy (Trick) and Trina.
  • Presented by Rockstar on October 8, 2026.
