Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album

34 original tracks, on November 19, 2026.

The official GTA VI album, announced by Rockstar and Atlantic Records on September 17, 2026: 34 original tracks, six already out, the rest on the game’s release day.

Official announcementApple MusicThe Leonida radio

Overview

Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records announced the album on September 17, 2026. It brings together 34 original tracks, written for the game, meant to “capture the electric energy of Vice City and Leonida”, according to the official message. It comes out on November 19, 2026, the same day as GTA VI, on streaming, CD and vinyl. Six tracks came out with the announcement, from Yung Lean to Keith Richards.

What the announcement says, and doesn’t say

On the same day, Rockstar promised to say more about the game’s music: a dynamic score and “the next evolution of in-game radio”. The album doesn’t say more: its tracks aren’t tied to any station, and nothing says yet when you’ll hear them while playing. Three physical editions are exclusive to the Atlantic Records site; the limited-edition vinyl sold out in minutes, according to TechRadar.

What we know for the game

For now, it’s an album to listen to outside the game. When Rockstar presents the stations, this page will say which ones play these tracks. The other known songs, from the trailers and the Extended Look, are in the radio player on the Radio and music page.

Key points

  • Announced on September 17, 2026 by Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records.
  • 34 original tracks, coming November 19, 2026, the day the game comes out.
  • Six tracks out with the announcement: Yung Lean, Travis Scott, CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, Morgan Wallen, Rauw Alejandro and Keith Richards.
  • No radio station is tied to these tracks for now.
