Back Country Radio

Country, outlaw country included.

Back Country Radio in GTA VI: country music, with Morgan Wallen (MW), Lana Del Rey (Delta Dawne) and reporter Mud Dog Mike. Presented on October 8, 2026.

Listen to the official previewThe six stationsThe Leonida radio

Overview

Back Country Radio is the game’s country station, outlaw country included. Its two hosts go by on-air names: singer Morgan Wallen is MW, and singer Lana Del Rey is Delta Dawne. A third character rounds out the team: Mud Dog Mike, a roving reporter and “swamp meteorologist”, played by Jeremy Dufrene, Lana Del Rey’s husband.

What the announcement says, and doesn’t say

Morgan Wallen is also on the game’s official album, with “Last Thing You Need”; Rockstar hasn’t said whether this track will play on Back Country Radio. The station’s playlist hasn’t been published.

What we know for the game

Like the other stations, it can be heard in a car and, for the first time in the series, on foot: with Jason’s or Lucia’s phone and earbuds, according to Rockstar.

Key points

  • Style: country, outlaw country included.
  • On the air: Morgan Wallen (MW) and Lana Del Rey (Delta Dawne).
  • Reporter: Mud Dog Mike, played by Jeremy Dufrene.
  • Presented by Rockstar on October 8, 2026.
  • Heard in the official preview, according to Game Informer: “Need a Little Time Off for Bad Behavior” (David Allan Coe), “Don’t Come Home a Drinkin’” (Loretta Lynn) and “The Conversation” (Hank Williams Jr. and Waylon Jennings).
