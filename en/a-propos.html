01

The starting point

Why Leonidakit exists

Before the release, rumors are everywhere and reliable numbers are nowhere.

The problem

A “likely” price is going around, so is a “leaked” map, and you can’t tell anymore what comes from Rockstar. Planning a session with that means counting on a price that doesn’t exist.

The promise

Here, every piece of info has a status and a dated source. What Rockstar hasn’t published stays “to be confirmed”, prices first. The calculator, the map, progress tracking and Léo then help you decide, with your own numbers.

03

The method

How we check

Five statuses, always shown, always linked to a source opened on a known date.

Official

Named or described by Rockstar, or official text reproduced by a named media outlet.

Example: Rideout Customs, named in the Ultimate Edition text of June 24, 2026.

Seen in media

Visible in a trailer, a screenshot or the Extended Look; price and purchase unknown.

Example: The tropical-pattern SMG, seen in the Vintage Vice City Pack screenshot.

Community identification

A match made by players, cited as such.

Example: The Karin Asterope, matched to a Toyota Camry by the community.

Series benchmark

What GTA V or GTA Online do, with their prices, never in the GTA VI column.

Example: Full armor at $5,000 in GTA V, recorded on the Bravado Gauntlet.

To be confirmed

Nothing published by Rockstar: the line says what we expect, not what is.

Example: The price of a haircut in GTA VI.

What we refuse, what we fix, when we check

What we refuse to publish

  • The 2022 and 2026 leaks, even when everyone’s talking about them.
  • A “likely” price or effect with no source.
  • An image that isn’t official.
  • Compatibility guessed from a silhouette.

How a fix is handled

You give the page, the problem and the source. The data is fixed in the source files, and the page is regenerated with the dated source. Without a source, the fix stays an open question.

09

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Five short questions, with answers you can check on the site.

Is Leonidakit an official site?
No. It’s a personal project, independent of Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive. The official images are credited in Media and credits.
Are the prices shown from GTA VI?
No. Rockstar hasn’t published any prices. The ones you see come from GTA V or GTA Online, marked “series benchmark”; in the calculator, they’re your numbers.
Where does the info come from?
From Rockstar’s website and Newswire, the trailers, the Extended Look, official texts picked up by the press and official screenshots; from GTA Wiki for the series. Every page lists its dated sources.
What happens to my data?
It stays in your browser: calculations, tracking, favorites, your conversation with Léo. Nothing leaves unless you send a form. Legal and privacy explains what’s stored and how to delete it.
How do I report an error?
Through the Contact page: the page, the problem, the source. The fix goes into the site’s data, and the page is regenerated with its dated source.
