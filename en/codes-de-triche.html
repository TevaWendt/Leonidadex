GTA VI cheat codes

No cheat codes have been announced for GTA VI. Here’s what we know as of October 4, 2026, how codes worked in GTA V, and the guide that will fill up as soon as a code is published by Rockstar or verified in-game.

Each piece of info says where it comes from: a Rockstar page, the press or a player guide. No assumed codes, no lists taken from the leaks: a GTA VI code will only go in here once Rockstar publishes it or it’s verified in-game. Understanding statuses.

For comparison

How codes worked in GTA V

GTA V codes, shown for comparison: they don’t work in GTA VI. Three ways to enter them, and rules written by Rockstar.

With the controller

During gameplay, you entered a sequence of inputs: D-pad, buttons and triggers.

Community identification12

On the in-game phone

You dialed a number starting with 1-999: the digits after it spell out the code’s name on the phone keypad. On PS3 and Xbox 360, only button inputs worked.

Community identification123

On PC, in the console

The ² key on a French keyboard, or ~, opens a console where you type the code’s name, like TOOLUP.

Community identification12

What Rockstar says about them

  • You can save with a code active, but achievements are disabled for a while.
  • Entering the same code twice usually cancels it, but not always.
  • Starting a mission turns off active codes; reloading a save or restarting the console cancels them all.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Are there cheat codes in GTA 6?
Nothing has been announced as of October 4, 2026: neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has talked about cheat codes for GTA VI, and neither the Extended Look nor the Game Informer cover story shows any. The “GTA 6” lists going around are speculation or come from the leaks: the site uses none of them. Since GTA III, every major GTA has had them; in GTA V, you entered them with the controller, on the in-game phone or, on PC, in the console.
Is there an infinite money cheat in GTA 6?
No. No codes have been announced for GTA VI, so there’s no money cheat either. In GTA V, no code gave you money, according to player guides; in San Andreas, HESOYAM gave $250,000 along with health and armor. To plan your purchases, the calculator works from your own numbers.
Do cheat codes disable trophies?
In GTA V, yes, for a while: Rockstar specifies that you can save with a code active, but that achievements are disabled; reloading a save or restarting the console cancels all codes. For GTA VI, nothing has been published: no codes, and no rule on trophies and achievements.
How do you enter a cheat code with the phone?
In GTA V, you opened the in-game phone and dialed a number starting with 1-999: the digits after it spell out the code’s name, like 1-999-866-587 for TOOLUP, the weapons. On PC, the console opens with the ² key on a French keyboard, or ~. For GTA VI, how any codes would be entered isn’t known.
Do cheat codes work online?
In GTA V, no: according to players, codes only worked in story mode, and GTA Online didn’t accept them. GTA VI is launching first as a single-player experience, with no codes announced.
