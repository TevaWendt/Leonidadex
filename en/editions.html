Standard or Ultimate

Two editions

Standard Edition

The game, digital or boxed.

  • The full game, digital or boxed
  • The pre-order bonuses, depending on how you buy
  • The Ultimate upgrade can be bought later, digitally

Ultimate Edition

The game and the Ultimate upgrade: sixteen extra items, spread across Jason and Lucia’s story, discovered chapter by chapter.

  • The full game, digital
  • Sixteen extra items, discovered as the story goes on
  • The same pre-order bonuses

In practice

Box, preloading and regions

The boxed version

  • The box contains a download code, not a disc.
  • It’s available from November 12, 2026, for delivery or pickup at the retailer, and its code lets you preload the game from that day.
  • There’s no boxed Ultimate Edition: with the boxed version, you buy the Ultimate upgrade digitally once the code is redeemed.

Preloading and release

  • Preloading starts on November 12, 2026 at midnight, local time.
  • Release is on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Codes and regions

  • PlayStation: a code is tied to an account country or region; the purchase must match the account’s region.
  • Xbox: codes aren’t limited to one region.
  • Japan: PlayStation codes expire 170 days after their issue date, which is set to launch day, November 19, 2026.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What’s in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?
The game plus sixteen extra items, including the ’95 Grotti Cheetah, the Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers, the Rideout Customs Mod Shop, the ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and Garage, and the Classic Car Collection.
Is there a disc in the GTA 6 box?
No: the box contains a download code, according to Rockstar Support. It arrives on November 12, 2026, the day preloading starts.
Can you upgrade from Standard to Ultimate?
Yes: the Ultimate upgrade is sold separately, digitally, on the PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store, once the game code is redeemed.
How long are the pre-order bonuses offered?
For a digital purchase made before November 20, 2026: the Vintage Vice City Pack and a month of GTA+. A boxed pre-order gets you the Vintage Vice City Pack.
