Rare and exclusive vehicles

What’s actually exclusive today: vehicles tied to an edition, and the ones whose name appears on just one official screenshot.

What “rare” means before release

Nobody knows yet how rare a vehicle is in the game: spawn rates, prices and unlock conditions aren’t public. What we can establish right now, though, is commercial exclusivity: vehicles tied to an edition or a pre-order pack, which not every player will get. And the models seen only once, in a single official image. This page lists both, and nothing else.

What you won’t find here. No list of “secret vehicles,” no spawn locations, no unlock methods. This information doesn’t exist before November 19, 2026, and any site publishing it today is making it up or taking it from the 2022 leak.

Frequently asked questions

Which vehicles are exclusive to the Ultimate Edition?
Rockstar names 8 vehicles on this edition’s product page: Crest Kayak, Shitzu Squalo, Dinka Enduro, Vapid Dominator Buggy ’67, Schyster Deviant, Vapid Riata Classic, Declasse Mamba GT, Grotti Cheetah ’95.
Are there secret vehicles in GTA VI?
No information of this kind is public before the game comes out. The secret vehicle lists going around come either from guesswork or from the September 2022 leak, whose content describes a much earlier development build and has never been confirmed.
How do you get the Vintage Vice City Pack vehicles?
This pack is pre-order content. The exact conditions for getting it, and whether you can get it later, are up to Rockstar, who hasn’t detailed them. We don’t pass on unofficial methods.
Are the Sirius, the Canis and the Ganado really in the game?
Their names are readable on screenshots Rockstar published in its official media kit. That’s the best proof available before release. However, neither the manufacturer of two of them nor their specs have been announced.
