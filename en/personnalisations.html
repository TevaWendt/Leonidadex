Customization

Everything you can customize on a vehicle or a weapon, item by item: what Rockstar has said or shown for GTA VI (Rideout Customs, One-Eyed Willie’s, the Ganado kit, engraved weapons, the Vintage pattern), and what the series already did, with its prices shown as benchmarks.

Official sources

What Rockstar has said and shown

Four moments, each with its status: the workshop screenshots, the June 24, 2026 editions, the Vintage Pack pattern, then the Rob Nelson interview. No prices, no list of options.

  1. June 24, 2026Official

    Two workshops unlocked with the Ultimate Edition

    Rideout Customs: “detailed interiors, exquisite rims, and donk stylings”. One-Eyed Willie’s, in Lake Leonida: “off-road modifications and hand-painted automotive artistry”. Both offer “a wide selection of artistic and performance-based vehicle mods”. The screenshots show a gold Albany on huge rims, a red interior and a hand-painted green Canis on all-terrain tires.

  2. June 24, 2026Official

    The Ganado retro kit and the engraved weapons

    Jason’s Vapid Ganado gets “exclusive mods including a cab spoiler, rear aerials, and special livery”. On the weapons side: “personalized sidearms with detailed engravings” for Jason’s Girardi ES9 and Lucia’s Klose K17, and a “his and hers” pair of Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers with “palm-tree-etched grips, engraved detailing, high-performance scope, and personalized finishes”. The Paradise and Shore Court garages have a weapon locker.

  3. June 24, 2026Official

    The Vintage Vice City Pack tropical pattern

    Pre-order bonus: a “tropical pattern available to adorn most guns”, inspired by Tommy Vercetti’s shirt. The screenshot shows it on an SMG with a folding stock and on a pistol; Rockstar doesn’t publish the gun-by-gun list.

  4. August 28, 2026Official

    Rob Nelson: Pay ’n’ Spray, workshops and lockers

    Speaking to Famitsu (translated September 9): at the Pay ’n’ Spray you repair and respray to shake off the police, but “you can basically only change the color there”, and elaborate paint is done “at a tuning shop”. Luxury cars have an alarm and a tracker, and a fence can remove it. Every Ammu-Nation has a locker for long guns, some stores sell exclusive weapons, and “there are also more weapon customization options than ever before”.

The two lists below sort all of this item by item, with the status and source of each row, then what Los Santos Customs, Benny’s, Ammu-Nation and the GTA Online weapon workshop offer, with their prices shown as benchmarks.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Can you customize your car in GTA 6?
Yes: Rockstar presents two shops, Rideout Customs (interiors, rims, donk style) and One-Eyed Willie’s (off-road, hand painting), with “a wide selection of artistic and performance-based vehicle mods”. Both are unlocked with the Ultimate Edition; no price is published. In GTA V, a turbo costs $2,500 and full armor costs $5,000.
Can you repaint your car in GTA 6?
Yes. According to Rob Nelson, the Pay ’n’ Spray repairs and repaints your car to shake off the police, but only the color changes: for a fancy paint job, you need a tuning shop. In GTA V, a classic paint job costs $400 and a chrome one $1,200.
Can you customize your weapons in GTA 6?
Rob Nelson promises “more weapon customization options than ever before”, with no details. Rockstar shows engraved pistols (Girardi ES9, Klose K17), Morgans with palm-tree-engraved grips and a scope, and the Vintage Pack’s tropical pattern on “most guns”. GTA V suppressors, magazines and scopes are listed as benchmarks.
What is the Ganado retro kit?
An Ultimate Edition exclusive kit for Jason’s Vapid Ganado pickup: cab spoiler, rear aerials and special livery, according to Rockstar. In the screenshot, the pickup is yellow with red stripes and has a hood scoop. No price or performance boost has been published.
Where are the mod shops and gun stores in GTA 6?
Rideout Customs is placed on the map in Crosstown (Vice City); One-Eyed Willie’s is at Lake Leonida according to Rockstar, with no verified location. Three gun stores are seen on the map: Phil’s Ammu-Nation, an Ammu-Nation with an assumed name in Rockridge, and Pawn & Gun in Port Gellhorn. Every Ammu-Nation has a locker for long guns.
