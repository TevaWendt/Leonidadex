05

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

What side activities are there in GTA 6?
Lots. The Game Informer cover story (September 29, 2026) lists base jumping, pool, gyms, kayaking, mini golf, diving, street and off-road racing, zoos, airboats and jet skis. On top of that come fishing and hunting at Mount Kalaga, store robberies, car theft and gang hideout raids, shown or described by Rockstar. Golf, tennis and bowling have never been announced.
Can you fish in GTA 6?
Yes. Rockstar presents Mount Kalaga as a fishing spot, and a screenshot shows Jason fishing from a boat. Rob Nelson of Rockstar North promises fishing as deep as in Red Dead Redemption 2, with one difference: you always release your catch, you neither keep it nor sell it. Fishing leads to challenges, collectibles and story moments; neither the species nor the rewards have been published.
Can you hunt in GTA 6?
Yes. Rockstar presents Mount Kalaga as a prime hunting ground, and the screenshots show armed hunters in the undergrowth. According to Rob Nelson’s comments as reported by content creator TGG, hunting earns money: you sell what you collect. Game Informer announces over 170 species, including some rare or legendary ones you can track. Neither the animals you’re allowed to hunt nor the prices have been published.
Can you go to the gym in GTA 6?
Yes. Rob Nelson confirmed it: there are gyms where you train to build muscle and strength, and exercise visibly builds Jason and Lucia’s muscles, just as what they eat affects their weight. The Extended Look shows Lucia on the bench press. The gym doesn’t earn you money, and no figures on its effects have been published.
Do side activities earn money?
Some do, according to Rockstar: robberies, gang hideout raids, stolen cars and goods sold to the fence, and hunting. Fishing doesn’t: catches are released. Rob Nelson adds that the story sometimes waits until you’ve saved up enough money. Rockstar hasn’t published any mission earnings or duration: the calculator’s activities are examples to replace with your numbers.
Can you rob stores in GTA 6?
Yes. Trailer 1 already shows Jason and Lucia in a liquor store, and the Extended Look strings together gas station robberies. Rob Nelson explains that you rob a store solo or as a pair, that jewelry stores and small banks are better guarded, and that destroying the security cameras keeps you from being seen. Part of the loot can be sold to the fence; no amounts have been published.
