The essentials

“Only in Leonida” pages: a moment in the GTA VI timeline noted in Leonidakit’s world files, May 6, 2025. Date: May 6, 2025. What this moment showed, page by page: Vice City (Rockstar presents it under the slogan “Everything in excess” and names Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka and VC Port); Leonida Keys (Rockstar calls it the “Gateway to paradise”: fishing, beach bars and small-time schemes, far from the bustle of Vice City); Grassrivers (Rockstar welcomes you with “Welcome to the wetlands”: alligators, airboats, hunting, fishing and poaching); Port Gellhorn (Slogan “Live hard”: cheap motels, empty malls, closed attractions, the place to lie low); Ambrosia (Slogan “Keeping Leonida sweet”: the Allied Crystal refinery provides the jobs, a local biker gang provides just about everything else); Mount Kalaga National Park (Slogan “Wild, wild country”: forests, gorges and rivers for hunting, fishing and off-roading); Jason Duval (his official profile: a youth among grifters, the army, work for the Keys smugglers and a life that keeps getting complicated); Lucia Caminos (her official profile: a father who taught her to fight, prison for defending her family, a mother who has dreamed of the good life since their Liberty City days); Cal Hampton (his official profile shows him happy at home, between beers, private tabs and Coast Guard radio: what if everything you read online were true?); Boobie Ike (his official profile: a local legend who turned his street years into a legal empire, with one bet left to win, Only Raw Records); and 14 more pages, listed below.
