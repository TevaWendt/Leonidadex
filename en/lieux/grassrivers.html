Grassrivers

Leonida’s wild swamps, where the alligator is the most famous attraction.

The country’s largest subtropical wilderness: mangroves, slow channels and flooded plains you cross by airboat. Rockstar calls it Leonida’s untamed jewel, as beautiful as it is dangerous.

View on the mapVehiclesOfficial page

Overview

Grassrivers is Leonida’s version of the Everglades: a huge subtropical wetland of mangroves, slow canals, flooded plains and cypress trees. Rockstar describes it as the state’s untamed jewel, as beautiful as it is dangerous, where alligators are the most famous attraction and where you mostly get around by airboat. Hunting, fishing and poaching sit side by side with wildlife protection units. The official screenshots show chases through the swamps, stilt shacks and locals posing with their catch in front of their workshop. It’s home turf for the Airboat 09 and the Vortex, and proof that you won’t explore the map only by car.

In the artwork and in the story

Rockstar presents Grassrivers as the largest subtropical wilderness in the country and as an untamed jewel: alligators, airboats, hunting and fishing, poachers and wildlife protection. The official screenshots show a chase by pickup and airboat through the swamps, locals posing with their catch in front of a workshop, stilt shacks and an aerial view of the canals.

On the ground

On the ground, you get around the region differently: by airboat, by kayak or on foot along the boardwalks. The Airboat 09 and Vortex Airboat pages are linked to it, along with the semi-automatic rifle and the double-barrel shotgun seen with its locals. The Leonidakit map marks its wetlands and water access points.

Key points

  • Mangroves, slow channels and flooded plains: the home turf of the airboats seen in the trailers.
  • Rockstar calls it the state’s untamed jewel, as beautiful as it is dangerous.
  • Hunting, fishing and poaching go on there right alongside wildlife protection units.
