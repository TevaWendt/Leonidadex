Region, GTA VI

Ambrosia

The state’s industrial heartland, where American industry and old-school values still rule.

Leonida’s farming and industrial side, built around the Allied Crystal sugar refinery. It provides the jobs; a local biker gang provides, according to Rockstar, pretty much everything else.

Read the fileView on the mapVehiclesGuess the LocationOfficial page

Overview

Ambrosia is the rural and industrial heart of the state: sugarcane fields, silos, straight roads and the smokestacks of the Allied Crystal refinery towering over the landscape. Rockstar sums up the region in one sentence: the refinery provides the jobs, the local biker gang provides everything else. The official screenshots show motorcycle convoys on country roads, biker gatherings, a woman biker brandishing a Mustang .357 revolver and parties in the mud. It’s an America of old values and old engines, far from the towers of Vice City, that Jason and Lucia pass through over the course of the story.

In the artwork and in the story

Ambrosia is the industrial countryside: the Allied Crystal refinery provides the jobs, the local biker gang provides the rest, as Rockstar puts it. The official screenshots show motorcycle convoys, a biker rally, a woman biker brandishing a Mustang .357 revolver and parties in the mud in the middle of the cane fields.

On the ground

On the ground, you cross the region on long straight roads, between silos and smokestacks; Sovereign motorcycles and pickups are right at home there. The Leonidakit map marks the refinery and the roads visible in the artwork; gang-related activities, if Rockstar reveals any, will be added to the page.

Key points

  • The Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides the region’s jobs.
  • A local biker gang provides, according to Rockstar, pretty much everything else.
  • Cane fields, silos and straight roads: a rural landscape the story passes through.
