Vice City

The capital of sun and excess, far from the ’80s but as hungry as ever.

Vice City brings together beaches, shopping districts, a cruise port and Downtown’s skyscrapers. Rockstar mentions Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka and VC Port, and makes it the densest, liveliest area in Leonida.

Read the fileView on the mapVehiclesOfficial page

Overview

A sprawling metropolis in southern Leonida, Vice City is home to pastel art deco hotels, clubs packed with celebrities and the biggest cruise port in the world. Rockstar presents it as a city where people, from Little Cuba to Tisha-Wocka, are always up for a party. Ocean Beach lines up its white sand and 1930s facades; Downtown raises its towers above the bay; VC Port welcomes the cruise ships. This is where Boobie Ike, Dre’Quan Priest and Real Dimez are building their music empire, between the Jack of Hearts and the Only Raw Records studio. On the map, the city holds most of the spotted places.

In the artwork and in the story

Vice City is the setting of both trailers: the beach at Ocean Beach, the Vice Beach pier, the palm-lined avenues and the Sahara Arena tower keep coming back from shot to shot. The official screenshots show everything from private jets over the giant sign to crowds on the sand, motorcycle convoys and night scenes under the neon. It’s where Boobie Ike built his empire and where Dre’Quan Priest lines up his parties.

On the ground

As in every GTA, the big city will pack in shops, workshops, salons and places to go out. On the Leonidakit map, Vice City already holds most of the 2,547 spotted places, from the Downtown towers to the motels on the outskirts; the Rideout Customs, Sara’s Unisex Salon, Stock 305, Electric Fang and Jack of Hearts pages are linked to it. Exact locations will fill in with the game.

Key points

  • Pastel art deco hotels and the white sand of Ocean Beach, the panaderías of Little Cuba: every neighborhood has its own signature.
  • Cruise port, Downtown skyscrapers and waterfront: the urban heart of Leonida.
  • This is where Boobie, Dre’Quan and Real Dimez are building their music empire.
  • The Jack of Hearts, Boobie’s club, is spotted on the map thanks to the official screenshots.

Reveal timeline

  1. December 4, 2023

    Trailer 1

    Seen in media

    The game opens on the city: the beach, the towers by the bay, Ocean Beach’s neon at night and modified cars.

  2. May 6, 2025

    Trailer 2

    Seen in media

    Around 0:37, the highway signs point to VCI Airport, Ambrosia, Downtown, Southside and Peacock Bay.

  3. May 6, 2025

    “Only in Leonida” pages

    Official

    Rockstar presents it under the slogan “Everything in excess” and names Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka and VC Port.

  4. June 24, 2026

    Ultimate Edition

    Official

    The edition texts name Stockyard (Stock 305, Electric Fang) and Southside, home of the PTT Youngin$ compound; the Squalo boat is docked at Washington Beach.

  5. June 24, 2026

    Vintage Vice City Pack

    Official

    The Shore Court garage, “a stone’s throw” from Ocean Beach, houses the ’55 Vapid Stanier; the pack’s screenshot puts Jason and Lucia outside the Ocean View Hotel.

  6. August 27, 2026

    An Extended Look

    Seen in media

    A police chase through the streets, a rooftop bar and a base jump off a skyscraper.

  7. August 27, 2026

    Press previews

    Official

    According to Rob Nelson, who presented the demo, Vice City alone is twice the size of Los Santos, GTA V’s city.

  8. Late August 2026

    29 new screenshots

    Official

    Three more screenshots: two towers by the bay, the waterfront at dusk and an armed man in the street.

  9. October 8, 2026

    The radio stations

    Official

    Flash FM is presented as “the Vice City institution” for pop, with Robyn and Alex on air.

Series benchmarks

What earlier games did along the same lines: benchmarks to get an idea, not information about GTA VI.

  • 1997

    Grand Theft Auto

    Vice City is already one of the three cities in the very first game, along with Liberty City and San Andreas.

  • 2002

    GTA Vice City

    Tommy Vercetti lands there in 1986: Ocean Beach, Washington Beach, Little Havana, Downtown. His first safehouse is a room at the Ocean View Hotel.

  • 2006

    GTA Vice City Stories

    Back to 1984 with Victor Vance, two years before Tommy: you build a business empire there, district by district.

What’s still to be confirmed

  • Which districts open from the start of the story, and which ones later?
  • Where exactly are Stockyard, Southside and Washington Beach in the city?
  • How many places can you go inside: shops, clubs, apartments?

Nobody has the answer yet: these points will be filled in with the game, on November 19, 2026.
