Vice City

The capital of sun and excess, far from the ’80s but as hungry as ever.

Vice City brings together beaches, shopping districts, a cruise port and Downtown’s skyscrapers. Rockstar mentions Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka and VC Port, and makes it the densest, liveliest area in Leonida.

View on the mapVehiclesOfficial page

Overview

A sprawling metropolis in southern Leonida, Vice City is home to pastel art deco hotels, clubs packed with celebrities and the biggest cruise port in the world. Rockstar presents it as a city where people, from Little Cuba to Tisha-Wocka, are always up for a party. Ocean Beach lines up its white sand and 1930s facades; Downtown raises its towers above the bay; VC Port welcomes the cruise ships. This is where Boobie Ike, Dre’Quan Priest and Real Dimez are building their music empire, between the Jack of Hearts and the Only Raw Records studio. On the map, the city holds most of the spotted places.

In the artwork and in the story

Vice City is the setting of both trailers: the beach at Ocean Beach, the Vice Beach pier, the palm-lined avenues and the Sahara Arena tower keep coming back from shot to shot. The official screenshots show everything from private jets over the giant sign to crowds on the sand, motorcycle convoys and night scenes under the neon. It’s where Boobie Ike built his empire and where Dre’Quan Priest lines up his parties.

On the ground

As in every GTA, the big city will pack in shops, workshops, salons and places to go out. On the Leonidakit map, Vice City already holds most of the 2,547 spotted places, from the Downtown towers to the motels on the outskirts; the Rideout Customs, Sara’s Unisex Salon, Stock 305, Electric Fang and Jack of Hearts pages are linked to it. Exact locations will fill in with the game.

Key points

  • Pastel art deco hotels and the white sand of Ocean Beach, the panaderías of Little Cuba: every neighborhood has its own signature.
  • Cruise port, Downtown skyscrapers and waterfront: the urban heart of Leonida.
  • This is where Boobie, Dre’Quan and Real Dimez are building their music empire.
  • The Jack of Hearts, Boobie’s club, is spotted on the map thanks to the official screenshots.
