Everything you can buy in GTA VI

Cars, weapons, clothing, housing, ammo, consumables: explore the listed categories and the documented ways to get things, section by section. A page or a category doesn’t prove you’ll be able to buy something. For each category, you see what the site already lists, what Rockstar has shown, and what’s still waiting for real data.

No separate in-game price is verified in the current catalog. Release is set for November 19, 2026. “To be confirmed” flags missing data; it’s not a promise that you’ll be able to buy it later. Understanding statuses.

01

Method

Reading the three statuses

Listed on the site

The section already has pages (vehicles, weapons, businesses, residences, safehouses), each with its certainty level and its source. The content exists in official media; nothing says you can buy it, and no price has been published.

Shown by Rockstar

Rockstar itself describes how you get the content, usually as an edition bonus (vehicles, garages, style collections, customization). The separate in-game price is still unknown.

To be confirmed

No verified entries for the category (housing, collectibles). The section is there to hold real data recorded in the game; it doesn’t promise anything.

The full rule, with examples, is in the Tutorial: “Understanding statuses”.

02

Official sources

What Rockstar has said about money and purchases

On June 24, 2026, Rockstar opened pre-orders on the Newswire: release on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, a single-player experience, and a Vintage Vice City Pack for purchases made before November 20, 2026. No in-game prices in that announcement.

The Ultimate Edition overview describes content and what it’s for: vehicles (Crest Kayak, Shitzu Squalo, Dinka Enduro, Dominator Buggy ’67), the Paradise garage in Watson Bay with “parking, a weapon locker and a drop-off for goods meant for a fence”, outfit and tattoo collections, and a mod kit for Jason’s Ganado. The Vintage Vice City Pack adds the Stanier ’55, the Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach, a retro suit and hairstyle, and a weapon pattern. No separate prices.

The official screenshots show the businesses: Stock 305 (streetwear), Sara’s Unisex Salon (hair, makeup, nails), Electric Fang Tattoo, Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie’s Mod Shop. These are announced services; nothing says the player will be able to own them.

The Extended Look from August 27, 2026 showed what hits your wallet: filling up and repairs at the gas station, the vehicle trunk that works as a locker, garages tied to properties with a drop-off for stolen goods, and a two-part inventory wheel. Still no amounts.

03

Benchmarks

What comes from earlier games and is still to be confirmed

For a sense of scale, the site cites GTA V: 252 vehicles at launch, before the hundreds added by GTA Online. No total has been published for GTA VI: the site’s counters (302 vehicles, 27 weapons) only count what’s visible in official material.

Eating, drinking and healing exist in the series; body armor and the medkit are among the gear shown for GTA VI. Their exact effects and their prices aren’t published. Same for the workshops and gun shops, spotted on the map with no catalog or price list.

Buying a home, a business or a garage separately: GTA V and GTA Online allowed it, GTA VI hasn’t confirmed it. The only garages described are tied to the editions. The 2022 and 2026 leaks aren’t used on this site.

04

Tools

Using it with the calculator

“My purchases”: choose a page, enter the price you imagine, and the calculator tells you if you’re short on money and how much play time you need. The price stays your guess until Rockstar publishes something.

“Is it worth it?” compares things with and without the purchase, over the same play time: handy for a business or a garage you assume makes money. “What should I buy first?” chains several purchases: the calculator waits until you have the money before each one and gives you the order and the time.

What you check off in each section shows up in your trackers on the Progress page (garage, wardrobe, properties…); empty categories don’t count toward the total.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Short answers, in plain words.

Why are there no prices?
No separate in-game price is verified in the current catalog. GTA VI is set to release on November 19, 2026. As soon as a price is known and verified, it will be added with its source. Until then, the calculator lets you enter the price you imagine.
What does “To be confirmed” mean?
That the site has no verified entries for this category. It confirms neither that it exists in GTA VI nor that you can buy it there. The section is ready and will be filled with real data recorded in the game, never with GTA V numbers.
Where do I check off what I’ve bought?
In each section: a vehicle in Vehicles, a weapon in Armory, documented content in its own section. It all shows up in Progress, with a percentage per category. Empty categories don’t count toward the total.
How do I work out if I can buy something?
Open the calculator, “My purchases” tool: choose the page, enter the price you imagine, and it tells you if you’re short on money and how much play time you need.
