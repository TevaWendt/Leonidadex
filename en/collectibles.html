Ultimate Edition

Find, restore, collect.

On the Ultimate Edition page, Rockstar presents an activity called Classic Car Collection: track down abandoned classic cars or unfinished projects across Leonida, then bring them back to their full glory. The job comes from Wyman, an eccentric collector and local mechanic. So far, it’s the only collection officially described.

The published screenshots show five models in his workshop. Each one has its page.

Method and sources

Rockstar hasn’t published any list of collectibles, any quantity or any location. This page says what’s established, by whom and since when, and how a page will enter the catalog once there’s evidence. Every claim has a status and links to a page checked on the date shown.

Official

A page or video published by Rockstar (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look), or statements by a Rockstar representative reported by a named outlet, with the date.

Seen in media

What an official image or clip shows without any Rockstar text saying it: a trailer shot, a screenshot, a moment from the Extended Look, with the timestamp when known.

Community identification

A match made by players, a wiki or a fan site (real place, actor, county). Quoted as such, never presented as confirmed.

To be confirmed

A question with no published answer: price, purchase, access, role in the story. We write it down so we don’t guess it.

How a page enters the catalog

Entering the catalog

  • A name, a category and at least one public, dated source.
  • Confirmed: a Rockstar page or video, or statements by a Rockstar representative reported by a named outlet.
  • Strongly established: several matching official elements (screenshot, clip, text), without written confirmation.
  • Unconfirmed: a lead flagged as such; it doesn’t count toward your progress and gets no location.

Verify

  • Rockstar first (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look, screenshots), then named outlets quoting Rockstar.
  • Each source keeps its URL, its publication date when there is one, and the date it was checked.
  • A location is only written with a verified point on our map (leonidakit-v1 system) and its source.
  • The 2022 and 2026 leaks are never used, not even for a lead.

Update

  • The review date is when the sources were last reread, not when the page was last put online.
  • After the November 19, 2026 launch, pages will come from the game itself, with screenshots and coordinates.
  • A page dropped from the sources is dropped from tracking; local checkmarks are kept but no longer count.
  • Corrections go through the contact form, with the page in question.
