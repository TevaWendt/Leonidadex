GTA VI trophies and achievements

The list of PlayStation trophies and Xbox achievements isn’t published yet. Here’s what we know as of October 4, 2026, what GTA V had, and your tracking, ready to light up with the list.

Every piece of info says where it comes from: store, console rule, press or trophy-hunting site. No “expected” list is used: a trophy only goes in here once PlayStation, Xbox or Rockstar publishes it. Understanding statuses.

What we know

GTA VI: the list isn’t published

As of October 4, 2026

Not published yet

Neither PlayStation, Xbox nor Rockstar has published the GTA VI trophy and achievement list: no count, no grades, no points, no names. Specialist sites like TrueAchievements and PlayStationTrophies.org have opened their pages and are waiting for it.

05

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

How many trophies are there in GTA 6?
The number isn’t published as of October 4, 2026: neither PlayStation, Xbox nor Rockstar has released the list. All we know is that the Xbox page announces achievements, and that Microsoft caps a game at 1,000 achievement points at launch. For comparison, GTA V had 51 trophies in its base game.
How do you get the GTA 6 Platinum trophy?
On PlayStation, the Platinum unlocks once you’ve earned all the game’s other trophies. For GTA VI, neither the list nor the Platinum has been published. A creator invited by Rockstar talks about a hundred hours or so, solo, but that’s not an official announcement. In GTA V, the “Los Santos Legend” Platinum also required GTA Online trophies.
Are there missable trophies in GTA 6?
Nothing is published: we’ll know once the list is out, and every missable trophy will be flagged here. In GTA V, PowerPyx counted six, mostly tied to the story’s final choice.
Will there be online trophies in GTA 6?
None have been announced. Rockstar presents GTA VI as a single-player experience at launch, and the list isn’t published. In GTA V, 34 of the 78 trophies were tied to GTA Online.
When will the trophy list be published?
No date has been announced. Specialist sites note that these lists usually show up a few days before release; GTA VI comes out on November 19, 2026, and the boxed version, a download code, arrives as early as November 12 for preloading.
How do I track my trophies on Leonidakit?
Each trophy will have its “Earned” box, saved on your device only, with the percentage and the progress ring. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and no account is needed.
