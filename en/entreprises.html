01

Official sources

What Rockstar has shown

Storefronts in the trailers, names in the official pages, then edition texts that describe five businesses.

  1. December 4, 2023Seen in media

    Trailer 1

    An Uncle Jack’s Liquor store robbed by Jason and Lucia (around 1:16) and a plane banner for the NINE 1 NINE club.

  2. May 6, 2025Official

    Pages and Trailer 2

    Boobie’s and Dre’Quan’s pages name the Jack of Hearts and Only Raw Records, Real Dimez’s label; Brian’s names his boatyard. Trailer 2 shows The Rusty Anchor bar (1:19), the NINE 1 NINE, the Jack of Hearts dancers (2:07) and an ad for “Phil’s Ammu-Nation” (2:10).

  3. June 24, 2026Official

    Editions

    Five businesses get an official text: Rideout Customs in Vice City (interiors, rims, “donk” style), One-Eyed Willie’s in Lake Leonida (off-road, hand-painted jobs), Stock 305 in Stockyard (streetwear), Sara’s Unisex Salon (hairstyles, beards, makeup, nails), Electric Fang Tattoo in Stockyard (more than 50 tattoos by the FAILE collective). The two shops and Stock 305 “are only open with the Ultimate Edition”. One mission hits the PTT Youngin$ store in Southside.

  4. August 27, 2026Seen in media

    Extended Look, Famitsu and after

    Gas station robberies, Lucia hiding her gun behind her back before walking into a store (17:57), shooting range, gym, the inside of the Jack of Hearts, the Effluvia bar. Rob Nelson adds in Famitsu: countless stores and salons, Ammu-Nation stores with a locker, a Pay ’n’ Spray that tells you what a stolen car is worth when resold, fences, well-guarded jewelry stores. Early September: filling up takes about ten seconds.

The eight pages above describe each storefront. This page says what’s written, what’s only been seen, and what’s missing.

05

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Can you buy businesses in GTA VI?
No, nothing announced. The businesses shown belong to characters (Boobie, Dre’Quan, Brian); Rockstar has only confirmed buying garages.
Are the Ultimate Edition stores exclusive?
Yes for Rideout Customs, One-Eyed Willie’s and Stock 305: in the Standard Edition, they “are not open”; an upgrade is sold separately. Sara’s offers “signature” hairstyles, Electric Fang “exclusive” tattoos.
Can you rob stores?
Yes. The Extended Look (August 27, 2026) shows gas station and store robberies, solo or with Lucia. According to Rob Nelson (Famitsu), jewelry stores and small banks are better guarded.
What is the Jack of Hearts?
Boobie Ike’s strip club, mentioned on his official page: the club’s money pays for the Only Raw Records studio. Seen in Trailer 2 and from the inside in the Extended Look; placed on our map.
Where can you fill up and repair your car?
At gas stations: according to Rob Nelson (early September 2026), filling up takes about ten seconds and the station also does repairs.

Sources and statuses

Every claim on this page has a status and comes from a page opened on the date shown. Rockstar pages are cited with the date they were last opened directly; their texts were reread on September 27, 2026 in the sources that reproduce them.

Official

A page or video published by Rockstar (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look), or statements by a Rockstar representative reported by a named outlet, with the date.

Seen in media

What an official image or clip shows without any Rockstar text saying it: a trailer shot, a screenshot, a moment from the Extended Look, with the timestamp when known.

Community identification

A match made by players, a wiki or a fan site (real place, actor, county). Quoted as such, never presented as confirmed.

To be confirmed

A question with no published answer: price, purchase, access, role in the story. We write it down so we don’t guess it.
