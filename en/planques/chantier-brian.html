Overview

Brian Heder’s boatyard is in the Leonida Keys, right on the water. Officially, boats get repaired and stored there; in reality, Rockstar describes Brian as still moving his goods through this workshop, with Lori, since the golden age of smuggling. The Brian’s Boat Works & Marina sign can be read above the workshop door in Trailer 2, around 1:25, at night, while something is being loaded. Jason and Cal work there for Brian. The boatyard is spotted on the map based on this scene.
