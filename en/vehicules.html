The count

What about the rest?

This list is incomplete, and that’s normal

Rockstar hasn’t published any total. For reference: GTA V had 252 vehicles at launch, before the hundreds added by GTA Online. Leonidakit lists 302 today, all spotted in official Rockstar media or identified by the community from that media.

Other sites announce higher numbers. Here’s where the gap comes from: they count vehicles seen in the 2022 and 2026 leaks. We don’t list them, because a vehicle seen in stolen code may never ship, and because passing that material around is neither legitimate nor useful to players.

The real count will be known on November 19, 2026. This page will be updated from the game itself, with the speeds, prices and locations every vehicle page is still missing.

Method

How to read this page

Rockstar hasn’t published any official vehicle list. So each entry shows its level of certainty, just like on our Leonida map.

Named by Rockstar

The name appears as is in official media: Rockstar’s website, the editions’ product page, or the Only in Leonida pages.

Seen in official media

The vehicle can be seen in Trailer 1, Trailer 2, the Extended Look from August 27, 2026 or an official screenshot, but Rockstar hasn’t given its name.

Community identification

The name or the real-world equivalent comes from a visual match made by players. It’s neither a confirmation from Rockstar nor an official license.

Our rule. No real-world brand or model is presented as confirmed by Rockstar: matches like “Grotti suggests Ferrari” are observations, never official statements. And nothing on this page comes from leaks.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How many vehicles are in GTA VI?
Rockstar hasn’t given any total. We list 302 vehicles here, 10 of which are named by Rockstar. The rest are either visible in official media with no name, or identified by the community.
Are there real car brands in GTA VI?
No. All the manufacturers are fictional, as in previous games. Matches with Ferrari, Porsche or Ford are player observations, not licenses.
Can you drive in first-person view?
Yes, first- and third-person views are shown in the official footage, with detailed interiors and working mirrors.
Do you need to refuel in GTA VI?
The official footage shows gas stations being used for fuel and repairs. So it really is a game mechanic, but exactly how it works is still unknown.
How do you get a vehicle?
Stealing one off the street is still the basic method, with two approaches shown: clone the key or smash the window. Luxury models require bypassing the immobilizer. Buying at a dealership and storing in a garage are also shown.
