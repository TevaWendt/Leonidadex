Overview

Jason’s house is in the Leonida Keys: a building on stilts by the water, owned by Brian Heder, who lends it to Jason in exchange for helping out with local debt collection. Trailer 2 shows him fixing a leaky roof before heading out to collect for Brian. Rockstar also presents it as his safehouse: the official gallery shows the vehicles parked out front, the yellow Ganado ’70, the Dinka Enduro and the Crest Kayak. It’s the starting point for the part of the story played in the Keys.

What we know about it

Jason’s house is a building on stilts by the water, in the Keys, owned by Brian Heder. Trailer 2 shows him fixing its roof before heading out to collect for Brian. The official gallery shows the vehicles parked out front: the yellow Ganado ’70, the Dinka Enduro and the Crest Kayak.

In the game

In the game, it’s the starting point for the part of the story played in the Keys, and probably Jason’s first garage. This page is linked to the three vehicles, to Brian Heder and to Jason’s safehouse, which is the name Rockstar gives the same house in its gallery.

Key points

  • Owned by Brian Heder, who lends it to Jason in exchange for helping out with local debt collection.
  • Rockstar also presents it as his safehouse: the vehicles parked out front appear in the official gallery.
