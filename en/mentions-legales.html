01

The essentials

In short

Five sentences to understand it all; the details follow, section by section.

  • No cookies, no trackers

    No account, no ads, no analytics, no scripts from other sites: so no cookie banner is needed. The only exception, if you choose it: the music player on the Radio and music page, loaded after your click. Checked in the code and in a browser on October 1, 2026.

  • Your data stays with you

    Calculations, progress tracking, map, collection and your conversation with Léo are saved in your browser and nowhere else.

  • What leaves, if you use it

    The Contact form and the home page alert send what you write in them, through Brevo. Your questions to Léo and to the calculator bar go to the site’s AI to be understood (the provider is named below). Nothing else is sent.

  • A personal project

    The site is published by a private individual, with no commercial activity, and hosted by Vercel.

  • Your rights

    To access your data, or have it corrected or deleted, write to contact [at] leonidakit [dot] com.

05

What stays, what leaves

Personal data

In plain terms Almost everything stays in your browser; only the two forms and your questions to the AI (Léo, calculator bar) leave, when you send them.

Data controller: the site’s publisher, a private individual (see Publisher), reachable at contact [at] leonidakit [dot] com. Their identity is known to the host (LCEN, Art. 1-1 II) and will be disclosed to you on request at this address, in particular so you can exercise your rights. The site has no data protection officer (not required for a personal site with no large-scale processing).

What stays in your browser

The tools save their data in your browser’s storage (localStorage, and sessionStorage for what is cleared when the tab closes). All keys start with “lk”. Nothing is written until you do something: a key appears with your first action (a number entered, a box checked, a draft, a selection, a question to Léo). The publisher cannot access this storage and never receives this data.

  • Calculator: your numbers, trackers, saved calculations, favorites and backup copies.
  • Tracking and Progress: what you checked off (vehicles, weapons, gear, lists), and your progress.
  • Map and Collectibles: places found, markers, drawn routes, layers, preferences, and for the trip calculator the vehicles you compare and the speeds you entered (lk_trajet_v1).
  • Ranking: your top 10.
  • Language: the language chosen with “Change language” or with the banner that offers it (a single key, kept until you choose another language); nothing is written if you don’t click.
  • Léo: the conversation stays in the tab (32 messages at most) and is cleared after 30 minutes of inactivity or with “Clear”. In local mode, Léo answers from your browser using the site’s files and nothing is sent; with the AI, your question is sent as described below.
  • AI (Léo and the calculator bar): your “Local” choice if you made it (lk_ia_mode), a random identifier created at your first question to the AI and sent with it to count your free questions (lk_ia_appareil), and the state of your access for the life of the tab (lk_ia_etat). The AI is entirely free: no credit, no payment.
  • Microphone (Léo and the calculator bar): nothing is written; the recognized text goes into the field, as if you had typed it.
  • Reporting: “Report a wrong answer” opens Contact with the question placed after the # sign, which the browser doesn’t send to the server; nothing is sent until you submit the form.
  • Contact: the form draft, cleared with “Clear the draft” or once the form is sent.

To erase: the “Clear” and “Clear all” buttons in the tools, or the browser setting that clears site data. To keep a copy: “Export my tracking” on the Progress page, and the calculator’s export. A shared calculation link contains the values you entered: only share it with people you choose.

06

Measured in the code

Cookies and trackers

In plain terms No cookies and no trackers: there is nothing to accept or refuse. The only exception, if you choose it: the music player.

Checked in two ways. First, in the shipped code (script outils/preuve-confidentialite.cjs, run at every check): no page sets a cookie (no document.cookie, no Set-Cookie header in the configuration or in the site’s functions), no page loads a script, stylesheet, font or image from another site, and no analytics or advertising tool is present; the font is hosted on the site. Then in a browser, on October 1, 2026, on 17 pages before any action and then during the main uses (calculator, map, lists, “I have it”, Léo, Contact draft, alert): 0 cookies, no IndexedDB database, no cache and no service worker, no request to another site. One exit from the site: submitting the home page alert, when you click “Notify me” after checking the box: your browser then goes to Brevo’s page.

Another exit, also at your request: on the Radio and music page, the “Listen here” button loads the official YouTube (Google, in privacy-enhanced mode) or Spotify player. Nothing from them loads before that click. Once the player starts, these services may set their own cookies, under their own rules. Closing the player stops it and removes it from the page.

The browser storage described above is used only for the features you use and is written only after an action on your part: it is exempt from consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, the loi Informatique et Libertés; the CNIL’s page “Cookies and trackers: what does the law say?”, its guidelines No. 2020-091 and its recommendation No. 2020-092 of September 17, 2020, consulted on October 1, 2026). That is why the site shows no banner: there is nothing to accept or refuse, and a banner with no trackers behind it would protect nothing.

When you sign up for the alert, you land on a Brevo page, which applies its own rules. Opening a link to another site (Rockstar, gtadb.org…) takes you off Leonidakit.

07

How reliable the info is

Liability and content

In plain terms The site always says where a piece of information comes from and how reliable it is; the calculations are estimates.

Each piece of information has a status (official, seen in media, community identification, series benchmark, to be confirmed) explained on the About page. A price or number from the game stays “to be confirmed” as long as Rockstar has not published anything.

The calculator’s results depend on the numbers you enter: they are estimates, with no guarantee that they match the game. Léo only says what the site says, with the source page; when the AI writes the answer, it relies only on the site’s pages, and this is stated under the answer. Léo can be wrong, and every answer has a link to report it.

Links to other sites are provided so you can check information; their content is the responsibility of their publishers. Found an error? Report it: it gets fixed as soon as a source allows.

08

Actual status

Accessibility

In plain terms The site is designed for keyboard use and reduced motion, but has not undergone an official audit.

What is done and checked by scripts with every version: full keyboard navigation with visible focus, text contrast at AA level (WCAG standards) on the checked pages, support for the “Reduce motion” setting, alt text for images, accessible names for form fields, announced error messages.

What is not done: no audit against the RGAA (the French accessibility standard), and therefore no compliance statement; no testing with an actual screen reader. If something blocks you, write to us describing your setup.
