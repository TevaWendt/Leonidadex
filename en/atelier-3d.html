      How the Workshop is built

      Models, not the game’s models

      Rockstar has released no 3D model. Each model is built from the page’s Leonidakit schematics: the side silhouette is stretched into a volume, narrowed by the top view, then gets its wheels, windows and lights. The proportions are those of the vehicle type, not of an exact model.

      Linked to Customization

      Every Workshop option matches a catalogue item (paint, wheels, spoiler, tinted windows, neons, suppressor, scope…). The link under the option opens its row in the Customization list, with its status and its series benchmark. The total adds up the known benchmarks; an option without one remains to be confirmed for GTA VI.

      Nothing leaves the device

      Saved configurations stay in this browser. The copied link only holds the chosen options. The PNG image is made here, by your browser.
