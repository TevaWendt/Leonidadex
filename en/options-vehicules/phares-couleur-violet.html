Purple

Purple: a value of the Colored headlights slot, recorded in GTA Online.

Series benchmarkColored headlights
The Colored headlights slotCustomization

The essentials

“Purple” is one of the 13 values of the Colored headlights slot (vehicle customization). Recorded in GTA Online with no price noted; nothing is published for GTA VI. Note: $20,000 to $80,000; half price after Arena War. The color dot shows the recorded shade. The other values of the slot: White, Blue, Electric blue, Mint green, Lime green, Yellow, Golden, Orange, Red, Pony pink, Hot pink, Blacklight.
