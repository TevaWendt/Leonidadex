Fowl Burger (Cluckin’ Bell)

Fowl Burger (Cluckin’ Bell) in GTA VI: Meals, series benchmark. Chicken burger from GTA IV that restores health; in GTA V, you can’t go into the restaurant anymore.

The essentials

Chicken burger from GTA IV that restores health; in GTA V, you can’t go into the restaurant anymore. Status: series benchmark (seen in another game, not yet in GTA VI). Effect: restores health (GTA IV). Series benchmark, GTA IV: 5 $ (soup 2 $, Cluckin’ Huge 4 $); this is not a GTA VI price. Where to find it: Fast food.
