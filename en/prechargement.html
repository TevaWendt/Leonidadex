Preload, November 12, 2026

Preloading GTA VI:
what’s certain, and what’s still pending.

GTA VI preloading starts on November 12, 2026 at midnight, local time, one week before the release on the 19th: that’s what Rockstar Support says. The download size and the unlock time on the 19th, on the other hand, haven’t been announced yet. This page says what’s official, how to get ready on PS5 and Xbox, and where the last numbers will drop.

02

Step by step

How to preload GTA VI on PS5 and on Xbox Series

What the PlayStation and Xbox support pages say about a pre-ordered game. Nothing specific to GTA VI has been published: these are the usual rules of the two consoles, and the PlayStation Store shows “preload 7 days early” on the game’s listing.

On PlayStation 5

  1. Pre-order on the PlayStation Store

    With the account that will play. The pre-order shows up right away in your Game Library, with a countdown.

  2. Check the automatic download box

    In the library, open the pre-ordered game, then its countdown: if the content’s box is checked, it downloads on its own as soon as it’s available.

  3. Leave the console in rest mode

    In the settings, turn on automatic updates and “Stay Connected to the Internet” in rest mode: the download starts without you, as soon as preloading opens.

OfficialPlayStation Support

On Xbox Series X|S

  1. Pre-order on the Microsoft Store

    On the console or on xbox.com, with the account that will play. Xbox codes aren’t region-locked.

  2. Automatic install in sleep mode

    A game you own installs on its own on your home console if it’s in sleep mode and “Keep my games & apps up to date” is turned on.

  3. Otherwise, from “My games & apps”

    My games & apps, Full library, Owned games: the game shows a download icon; select Install. Never buy the game again if a price shows up instead of Install.

OfficialXbox Support

With the box, it’s the same thing once the code is entered in your console’s store. Preloading opens at midnight, local time, on November 12: the console handles it overnight if it’s in sleep mode, otherwise you start it yourself the next day.

The boxed version

What if I buy GTA VI boxed?

Three things to know, all stated by Rockstar Support.

  • A code, not a disc

    The box contains a download code. The game downloads like a digital version, with the same file size.

    OfficialRockstar Support

  • From November 12

    The box is available from November 12, 2026, for delivery or pickup, so you can preload the game before release.

    OfficialRockstar Support

  • No boxed Ultimate

    The Ultimate Edition doesn’t exist as a boxed version: the upgrade is bought digitally once the game’s code is redeemed.

    OfficialRockstar Support
