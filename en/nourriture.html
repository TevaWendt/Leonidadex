GTA VI · documented content

Consumables

Eating, drinking, healing, protecting yourself: consumables help you get health back and hold out during a mission. Here’s the full list of what we know for GTA VI, along with what the series did before.

Rockstar hasn’t published any prices or effect numbers for GTA VI: each row shows its status, and numbers from GTA V, GTA IV or San Andreas stay in their benchmark column.

01

Official sources

What Rockstar has said and shown

Four moments, each with its status: what the game shows on screen and what Rob Nelson said afterward. Nothing comes with numbers: no prices, no amount of health restored.

  1. May 6, 2025Seen in media

    Official screenshots: bars and pools

    The screenshots released with Trailer 2 show Jason with beers at a bar counter and Lucia with a cocktail by a pool; The Rusty Anchor, a beach bar in the Keys, shows up in another image. An image shows an item, not its price.

  2. August 27, 2026Seen in media

    Extended Look: eating, drinking, robbing

    During the 26 minutes, Jason and Lucia eat tortilla chips in the street, stop by the Effluvia bar on a Vice Beach rooftop, go to the gym and rob gas stations wearing bandanas. A burger ad plays on the apartment TV; cans of Sprunk sit on the shelves of a Xero station.

  3. August 27, 2026Official

    Rob Nelson: food affects weight

    On the day of the broadcast, VGC and then Dazed (September 6) published the words of Rob Nelson, head of development: “Food affects Jason and Lucia’s weight, while exercising visibly builds and tones their muscles”. Nights without a bed, life on the run and a bender lasting several days also show on their faces.

  4. August 28, 2026Official

    Famitsu: morning coffee, protein shake and protein bar

    Speaking to Famitsu, Rob Nelson mentions morning coffee, then the protein shake and protein bar after a workout: “Food provides buffs that strengthen your meters”. Three days of training in a row raise health and strength. No items, no prices and no effect numbers have been published.

What this means for the list: nine rows are confirmed for GTA VI (named by Rockstar or seen in official media) and none of them has a price. The rest comes from the series, and each row says so.

Benchmarks

What the series already did

The benchmarks come from GTA V, GTA Online, GTA IV and San Andreas. They show what players expect, not what GTA VI will do: that’s why they stay in their own column.

Eating and drinking to recover

  • GTA V: a can of eCola or Sprunk from a vending machine, $1, restores full health
  • GTA V: P’s & Q’s $1, EgoChaser $2, Meteorite $4 at convenience stores
  • GTA V: health refills on its own up to half while standing still
  • GTA IV: stands and fast-food places restore health; Bleeder burger for $1

Protect yourself

  • GTA V: five armor vests at Ammu-Nation, from 20% ($500) to 100% ($2,500)
  • GTA Online: the same vests, from $100 to $500, unlocked by rank
  • Health kit: free, picked up around the city (San Andreas, GTA IV)

Weight and fitness

  • San Andreas: a Cluckin’ Bell meal adds 1 to 3% fat, the salad 0%
  • San Andreas: eating too much makes you throw up
  • GTA VI: Rob Nelson confirms that weight and visible muscles are back

Each benchmark shows its source in the list, in the “Series benchmark” column. When a page we checked doesn’t give a number for an effect, the row says so instead of making one up.

08

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Can you eat in GTA 6?
Yes. Rockstar shows Jason and Lucia eating and drinking (chips in the street, morning coffee, the Effluvia bar), and Rob Nelson says food strengthens the gauges and affects weight. No list of items or prices has been published.
Do you gain weight in GTA 6?
Yes, according to Rob Nelson: food affects weight, eating too much makes you gain weight, the gym visibly builds muscle, and sleepless nights or benders show on the face. Just like in San Andreas, where a meal added 1 to 3% fat.
How much does a soda or a snack cost in GTA 6?
Rockstar hasn’t published any prices. Series benchmark: in GTA V, a can from a vending machine costs $1 and restores full health; P’s & Q’s $1, EgoChaser $2, Meteorite $4 at convenience stores.
Is there body armor in GTA 6?
Not confirmed yet. Ammu-Nation exists in GTA VI (weapon locker, exclusive weapons), but Rockstar hasn’t shown or named any vest. In GTA V, five vests range from 20% ($500) to 100% ($2,500).
Can you drink alcohol in GTA 6?
Rockstar shows Jason with a beer at the bar and Lucia with a cocktail, and the Extended Look stops by the Effluvia bar. Rob Nelson adds that a bender lasting several days shows on the character. Prices and effects haven’t been published.
