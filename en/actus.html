Frequently asked questions

What’s the latest official announcement about GTA 6?
On October 15, 2026, Rockstar released the launch trailer, set to “Flower in a Hurricane”, Justice’s new single. This page’s timeline is updated with every announcement.
When does GTA 6 come out?
On Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Preloading starts on November 12, 2026, according to Rockstar Support. Preloading, step by step
How much does GTA 6 cost?
€79.99 for the Standard Edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate Edition on the PlayStation Store in France, as of October 16, 2026. Retailers set their own prices; the prices page has the details, along with the collector’s box.
How many official GTA 6 videos are there?
Four: Trailer 1 (December 4, 2023), Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025), An Extended Look (August 27, 2026) and the launch trailer (October 15, 2026). Each one has its own page on the site.
Does this page include the info going around on social media?
No. Only Rockstar’s publications, and press articles that quote them, are included. The stolen videos from 2022 and August 2026 are never used.
Does the GTA 6 collector’s box include the game?
No. “The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection” brings together eleven items, without the game, which is sold separately. It’s a limited edition on the Rockstar Store and arrives on November 19, 2026.
