The essentials

Water plays a big part in Leonida. Rockstar confirmed jet skiing, kayaking, diving and airboats, and the images place them: a Seashark jet ski off Vice City in Trailer 2, divers on the reefs of the Keys, airboats in the Grassrivers swamps, a Crest kayak at Jason’s safehouse. Nothing has been published yet about possible challenges or rewards.

What Rockstar has said and shown

  1. The Game Informer cover story lists jet skis, kayaking, diving and airboats among the activities confirmed by Rockstar.

Where to do it

The Keys for diving and jet skis, Grassrivers for airboats, the coast and rivers of Mount Kalaga for kayaking, based on the screenshots.
