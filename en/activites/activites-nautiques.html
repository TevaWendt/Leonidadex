  • The Game Informer cover story lists jet skis, kayaking, diving and airboats among the activities confirmed by Rockstar.

Where to do it

The Keys for diving and jet skis, Grassrivers for airboats, the coast and rivers of Mount Kalaga for kayaking, based on the screenshots.
