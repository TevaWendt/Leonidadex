The essentials

Robbing a store is one of the ways to make money in GTA VI: Trailer 1 already shows Jason and Lucia in a liquor store, and the Extended Look runs through gas stations one after another. You can go alone or as a pair, Rob Nelson confirms. Jewelry stores and small banks are better guarded than shops, and breaking the security cameras helps you get away unseen. The loot is then sold to a fence.

Where to do it

Rockstar hasn’t published the list of stores you can rob. The only one identified on screen is the Uncle Jack’s Liquor from Trailer 1, which the players’ map places in Port Gellhorn.

What it pays

Cash, and sometimes loot to sell: garages have a stash for stolen goods, which a fence buys for less than they’re worth. Neither what’s in a register nor the discount rate has been published.
