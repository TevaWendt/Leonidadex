The essentials

In GTA VI, stealing a car is no longer a single button press. Depending on the model, you have to break the window, slide in a slim jim or clone the key, and some tools are only unlocked as the story progresses. Luxury cars also have an alarm and a tracker: a fence can deal with them, and Pay ’n’ Spray tells you what the car is worth before you sell it.
