The essentials

Fishing in GTA VI aims to be as complete as in Red Dead Redemption 2, according to Rob Nelson. Rockstar places it in Mount Kalaga, and a screenshot shows Jason fishing from a boat, Cal with binoculars. One big difference: every catch is released. So fishing brings in nothing from resale; it serves challenges, collectibles and story moments.

What Rockstar has said and shown

  1. The official Mount Kalaga National Park page makes it a spot for fishing, hunting and off-roading.

    Official1

  2. An official screenshot shows Jason fishing from a boat, with Cal on binoculars beside him.

    Seen in media2

  3. Rob Nelson, speaking to content creator TGG at Rockstar North, promises fishing as deep as in Red Dead Redemption 2.

    Official3

  4. Catches are always released: keeping them “didn’t fit this game”, he says. Fishing is tied to challenges, collectibles and story moments.

    Official34

Where to do it

Mount Kalaga, according to Rockstar; at sea from a boat, according to the screenshot. No fishing spot has been placed on the map yet.
