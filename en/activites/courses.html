At night in the city, in the mud up north.

The essentials

Two moods for speed: night on the streets of Vice City, where the Extended Look shows modified cars racing, and the mud of Mount Kalaga, whose off-road trails Rockstar promises. Street and off-road racing are confirmed in Game Informer’s cover story, which also mentions motorcycle races, a stock car and a rally car on a track. Prizes and rankings have not been published.

What Rockstar has said and shown

  1. Street and off-road racing is on the list of confirmed activities in the Game Informer cover story.

  2. The official Mount Kalaga page promises off-road trails.

    Official2

  3. According to Game Informer, the Extended Look shows modded cars racing at night, motorcycle races, a stock car and a rally car on a track.

  4. The screenshots show dirt bikes and a quad in the mud at Mount Kalaga, and a mud party in Ambrosia, among buggies and quads.

Where to do it

The streets of Vice City for night races, Mount Kalaga for off-roading. The players’ map also marks racetracks, like the Gellhorn International Raceway and the Boone Speedway.
