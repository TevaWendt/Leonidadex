Official today

What Rockstar has confirmed

The official announcements, rechecked on October 17, 2026. Each point links to the site page that covers it in detail, and to its source.

  • Date and consoles

    GTA VI comes out on Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar announced this date on November 6, 2025, when the game was delayed for the second time.

    The countdownOfficial source ↗

  • Pre-orders and editions

    Pre-orders have been open since June 25, 2026. Two editions: Standard and Ultimate; the Ultimate Edition adds sixteen items, including the Grotti Cheetah ’95, the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers and the Classic Car Collection.

    What the editions includeOfficial source ↗

  • Standard€79.99Ultimate€99.99

    The game’s price

    On the PlayStation Store in France: €79.99 for the Standard Edition, €99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. For the boxed version, each retailer sets its own price.

    The pricesOfficial source ↗

  • Pre-order bonus

    Before November 20, 2026, a pre-order or a digital purchase also gets you the Vintage Vice City Pack (the Vapid Stanier ’55 and its garage, outfits and hairstyles, a weapon pattern) and a month of GTA+; a boxed pre-order gets you the pack.

    The Stanier ’55Official source ↗

  • The boxed version

    The box contains a download code, not a disc. It arrives in stores from November 12, 2026, the day preloading starts. There’s no boxed Ultimate Edition.

    The boxed versionOfficial source ↗

  • The characters

    Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval are the two protagonists. Rockstar has also introduced Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre’Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder.

    The charactersOfficial source ↗

  • Leonida

    The story takes place in the State of Leonida, inspired by Florida: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

    The regionsOfficial source ↗

  • The videos

    Four official videos: Trailer 1 (December 4, 2023), Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025), An Extended Look (August 27, 2026), captured entirely in-game on PlayStation 5, and the launch trailer (October 15, 2026).

    The official videosOfficial source ↗

  • The music

    Six radio stations presented on October 8, 2026, with their hosts, on-demand podcasts and a radio you can also listen to on foot. The official album, 34 original tracks, comes out on November 19, 2026.

    Radio and musicOfficial source ↗

  • The collector’s box

    The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection: eleven items inspired by Macca the Gator, without the game, in a limited edition on the Rockstar Store ($399.99), shipping from November 19, 2026.

    The box in detailOfficial source ↗

  • The limited-edition controllers

    Two DualSense controllers in GTA VI colors, one black and one white, at a suggested price of €84.99 each, from November 19, 2026, presented by PlayStation.

    The controllersOfficial source ↗

  • The Rockstar Store collection

    Since October 8, 2026, the Rockstar Store has been selling the Grand Theft Auto VI Collection: T-shirts, caps, keychains, a money clip, a Zippo lighter, tote bags and stickers.

    The items and their pricesOfficial source ↗

04

On the pages

What the pages need from the game

Each vehicle or weapon page says what’s already known, then what’s still to be measured. Here’s why these fields are waiting for November 19, explained once here rather than on every page.

1How to get it
Price, purchase, how to get it and location. None has been published. The dealerships, gun stores and shops on our map are places spotted in official media, not confirmed places to buy a specific model.
2Performance and firing
Speed, acceleration, braking, handling; for weapons, range, fire rate, accuracy, reload and magazine. No measurement exists before the game, and an “estimated” range on a weapon page comes from the real weapon that inspired it.
3Capabilities
Seats and cargo are waiting for the game. When a page shows a terrain (road, water, air), it comes from the vehicle’s category, not from a spec sheet.
4Customization
The options on each page come from the customization catalog: what Rockstar has shown for GTA VI, and GTA V and GTA Online benchmarks flagged as such.

Running costs, item by item

Fuel exists in the game: you can fill up at the gas stations shown in the Extended Look. But no price or fuel consumption has been published, and nothing has been announced for the other costs. The calculator can simulate them if you pick an assumption.

Fuel
A gauge seen in a trailer doesn’t prove a price, fuel consumption or a running cost.
Maintenance
No vehicle maintenance cost has been announced for GTA VI.
Repairs
Paid repairs in GTA V prove nothing for GTA VI.
Insurance
No vehicle insurance has been announced for GTA VI.
Resale
A resale only counts if it’s possible and its price is known or chosen as an assumption. It never lowers the money you pay today.
Ammo used
Ammo prices and the amount fired per mission aren’t published.
Healing per consumable
Rockstar says food affects the gauges, but gives no numbers: putting a number on healing takes a known amount and a known reference.
Business income
No business income, stock or production cap has been published for GTA VI.
Mission failure
No success rate can be backed up: we compare scenarios (everything succeeds, one failed attempt…) instead of making up a rate.
Temporary bonus
A bonus only counts if its conditions and duration are known or chosen as an assumption.

As soon as a value is published or measured in-game, it shows up on the page with its source and its date.

05

Matches

Real-world models and provisional names

What you read on a page when Rockstar hasn’t said anything.

  • Inspired by real models, never licensed

    Rockstar doesn’t reproduce any real-world model under license: its vehicles and weapons are inspired by real ones, and the studio doesn’t say which. When a page names a real-world model, it’s the site’s own observation.

  • Matches, not announcements

    Our descriptive names and the real-world inspirations are matches made from the official images, not information shared by Rockstar.

  • A provisional name in the meantime

    A vehicle or weapon model seen in official media keeps a provisional name, taken from the series or from its real-world model, until Rockstar names it.

Most reliableWaiting for the game

  1. Official

    A page or video published by Rockstar (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look), or statements by a Rockstar representative reported by a named outlet, with the date.

  2. Seen in media

    What an official image or clip shows without any Rockstar text saying it: a trailer shot, a screenshot, a moment from the Extended Look, with the timestamp when known.

  3. Community identification

    A match made by players, a wiki or a fan site (real place, actor, county). Quoted as such, never presented as confirmed.

  4. To be confirmed

    A question with no published answer: price, purchase, access, role in the story. We write it down instead of guessing.

06

What we refuse

What we never use

What the site never does, no exceptions.

  • The 2022 and 2026 leaks, even when everyone’s talking about them.
  • A “likely” price or effect with no source.
  • An image that isn’t official.
  • Compatibility guessed from a silhouette.
