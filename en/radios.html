What Rockstar has shown

Rockstar hasn’t presented any stations. It has featured songs in three videos, released an album and promised a revamped radio.

  1. December 4, 2023Official

    Trailer 1

    The whole trailer plays over “Love Is a Long Road” by Tom Petty, credited in the official video’s description.

  2. May 6, 2025Official

    Trailer 2

    “Hot Together” by the Pointer Sisters carries the video; three car radio snippets play while Jason drives through the Keys.

  3. August 27, 2026Seen in media

    Extended Look

    Fourteen licensed songs, plus original synth music in the tense moments, according to press findings.

  4. Fall 2026Official

    Dazed

    Rupert Humphries, from Rockstar’s narrative team, sums up the musical spirit: “We love Miami music, particularly Miami bass”. He names Kodak Black and Sexyy Red.

  5. September 17, 2026Official

    The album and the radio

    Announcement of “Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album”, with Atlantic Records. The Newswire also promises a dynamic score and “the next evolution of in-game radio”, with no details.

A “seen” song was heard in an official video and recognized by several media outlets, with no credit published by Rockstar.

05

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Which radio stations will be in GTA 6?
No station has been announced so far. Rockstar only promises “the next evolution of in-game radio” and a dynamic score (September 17, 2026). The station names going around come from stolen videos from August 2026: we don’t use them.
What’s the music in Trailer 2?
“Hot Together” by the Pointer Sisters (1986), credited by Rockstar. You also hear short car radio snippets while Jason drives: Zenglen, Wang Chung and Tammy Wynette, noted by the press.
Who sings in Trailer 1?
Tom Petty, with “Love Is a Long Road”, from the album Full Moon Fever (1989). It’s the only song in the video, credited in its official description.
What songs can you hear in the Extended Look?
Fourteen licensed songs, from Birdman and Lil Wayne to Phil Collins, by way of Def Leppard, Depeche Mode and Cliff Richard. The press listed them with their timestamps; Rockstar hasn’t published the list.
What is “Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album”?
The game’s official album, announced by Rockstar and Atlantic Records on September 17, 2026: 34 original tracks, coming out on November 19, 2026 with the game. Six tracks are already out, including those by Travis Scott, Yung Lean and Keith Richards.
Are the station names going around confirmed?
No. These names come from the radio menu in stolen videos from August 2026, not from a Rockstar presentation. The site doesn’t use them and is waiting for the official station announcement.

Official

A page or video published by Rockstar (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look), or statements by a Rockstar representative reported by a named outlet, with the date.

Seen in media

What an official image or clip shows without any Rockstar text saying it: a trailer shot, a screenshot, a moment from the Extended Look, with the timestamp when known.

Community identification

A match made by players, a wiki or a fan site (real place, actor, county). Quoted as such, never presented as confirmed.

To be confirmed

A question with no published answer: price, purchase, access, role in the story. We write it down so we don’t guess it.
