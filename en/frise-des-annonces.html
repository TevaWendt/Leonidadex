        01
        The rules
        How to play
        1. Five announcements a day
          The draw changes at midnight, Paris time. A first announcement is already on the timeline, with its date: that’s your starting point.
        2. A place for each announcement
          Read the announcement, then tap the spot on the timeline where it goes: before, between or after the ones already there.
        3. The answer, right away
          The announcement slides into its real place, with its date, its status, its source and the link to its page on the site.
        4. Your streak, then free play
          Finish the daily game to extend your streak and earn your points. Then play as much as you like, at random.
        

        02
        Where the announcements come from
        Only dated, sourced announcements
        The game uses the announcements from the News page: what Rockstar published, and what the press reported.
        • 29
          announcements in the game
        • 20
          official announcements
        • 9
          reported by the press
        Every announcement has a date, a status and a source. “Official”: published by Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft on their own channels. “Seen in media”: reported by the press, which is named.
        A game never has two announcements from the same day: each announcement has only one right place. The announcements are spread over cycles where each one comes up once, and none comes back the next day.

        03
        Questions
        Frequently asked questions
        Do the announcements in the Timeline come from leaks?
        No. Only what Rockstar and its partners published, and what the press reported with a date and a source. The site uses nothing from the leaks.
        What does “Seen in media” mean?
        That the information comes from the press, not from a Rockstar channel: an interview, a preview or an investigation. The source names the outlet.
        Is my score saved anywhere?
        Only on your device, in your browser’s storage. Nothing is sent; sharing only passes the text of your result to the app you choose.
        Will new announcements be added?
        Yes: every new announcement on the News page joins the game, with its date and its source.
