Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Will there be a GTA Online for GTA 6?
Nothing has been announced as of October 4, 2026. Rockstar presents GTA VI as a single-player experience, out November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Take-Two only talks about that single-player side. Twitch’s boss mentioned multiplayer in 2027, without confirmation from Rockstar. GTA V’s GTA Online, meanwhile, keeps going.
When will GTA 6 Online come out?
No date: Rockstar hasn’t even announced an online game for GTA VI. Twitch’s boss, Dan Clancy, mentioned 2027 to Bloomberg; neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed it. For comparison, GTA Online arrived two weeks after GTA V, in 2013.
Will my GTA Online character transfer to GTA 6?
Nothing has been announced: no character, no money, no assets, no rank. The only precedent is within GTA V: the Enhanced version on PC, in March 2025, let you carry over your progress. Take-Two says it will keep today’s GTA Online going after GTA VI’s release.
Will today’s GTA Online keep going?
Yes, according to Take-Two: on September 17, 2026, the publisher said again that it would support GTA Online “after GTA 6’s release and beyond”. In August, Strauss Zelnick talked about a steady pace of updates; The Kortz Center Heist, released on July 14, 2026, is one of the latest.
What’s the free month of GTA+ with the pre-order for?
For today’s GTA Online, not GTA VI: a digital pre-order made before November 20, 2026 gives you a month of GTA+, to activate before March 31, 2027 on PS5, or within 180 days on Xbox. It then renews at the regular price unless you cancel it.
Do you need PlayStation Plus or Game Pass to play GTA 6?
Nothing suggests so: GTA VI is announced as a single-player game, and its PlayStation page says it can be played offline. No subscription is mentioned to play it.
