Character, GTA VI

Brian Heder

Nothing beats a Mudslide at sunset.

An old-school trafficker from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys, Brian still moves his goods through his boatyard with Lori, his third wife. He puts Jason up in exchange for a few favors.

Read the fileOfficial page

Overview

Brian Heder is an old-school trafficker, shaped by the golden age of smuggling in the Keys. Rockstar describes him as a Leonida beach bum with the bite of a great white shark: long enough in the business to let others do the dirty work. He still moves his goods through his boatyard, with Lori, his third wife. He lets Jason live for free in one of his properties, as long as he helps with local debt collection and stops by for Lori’s sangria. In his view, nothing beats a Mudslide at sunset. The screenshots show him riding with Jason, leaning on a railing, holding a package in a plane and handing Jason a pistol.

In the story

In Trailer 2, Brian is Jason’s boss: he’s the one who sends him out to collect, and Jason’s day starts in front of his boatyard. The screenshots show him in a car with Jason, leaning on a railing, and handing Jason a pistol in front of a pickup. Rockstar ties him to the golden age of smuggling in the Keys, which makes him the old-world character set against Jason’s and Raul’s ambitions.

In the screenshots and on the pages

The official screenshots show him riding with Jason, leaning on a railing with two cans, holding a package in a plane, and handing Jason a pistol in front of a pickup. His page links to the Caracara 4x4, the boatyard spotted on the map, his house in the Keys, Jason and Cal.

Key points

  • An old-school trafficker, from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys.
  • Still moves his goods through his boatyard, with Lori, his third wife.
  • Long enough in the business to let others do the dirty work.
  • Puts Jason up for free, in exchange for a hand with debt collection and stopping by for Lori’s sangria.
