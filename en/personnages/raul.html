Raul Bautista

Experience counts.

A seasoned bank robber, Raul recruits partners ready to take the risks that pay the most. His recklessness raises the stakes with every job; his crew will have to keep up or get out.

Official page

Overview

Raul Bautista is an experienced bank robber who relies on confidence, charm and cunning. Rockstar describes him as always on the lookout for talent ready to take the risks that pay off the most, and warns that his recklessness raises the stakes with every score: his crew will have to keep up or bow out. Experience counts, says his official introduction. The screenshots show him on the phone in front of screens, behind the wheel of a car with a custom pistol, and on the roof of a Vice City building. He’s the one who brings Jason and Lucia into the most ambitious scores.

In the story

Raul is the story’s bank robber: the one who recruits, plans and pushes the others to take risks. The official screenshots show him in a light-colored suit on a Vice City rooftop, on the phone in front of surveillance screens, and behind the wheel, pistol in hand. His introduction casts him as the driving force behind the big scores, with a warning from the studio about his recklessness: he’s the one raising the stakes for Jason and Lucia.

In the screenshots and on the pages

The official screenshots show him on the phone in front of screens, behind the wheel of a car with a custom pistol in hand, in a suit on a Vice City rooftop, and in front of a bar’s neon lights. His page is linked to the custom pistol and to Jason and Lucia, whom he pulls into the most ambitious scores.
