An Extended Look

A presentation of about 26 minutes, aired on August 27, 2026, first on Netflix and then on YouTube, captured on PlayStation 5.

  • Official

    Rockstar announces it as a presentation of about 26 minutes, first on Netflix and then on YouTube. 1

  • Official

    It covers the gym, basketball, the shooting range, races, car theft, robberies, the police and Jason and Lucia’s daily life. 12

  • Seen in media

    The press has listed fourteen licensed songs in it, with their timestamps; Rockstar hasn’t published the list. 12
