Trailer 1 for GTA 6

The first GTA VI video, released on December 4, 2023: Lucia, the State of Leonida and Vice City, over “Love Is a Long Road” by Tom Petty.

  • Seen in media

    The video opens on Lucia, questioned in an orange jumpsuit at the Leonida Penitentiary. 1

  • Seen in media

    Jason appears at her side: a motel room, a car getaway, a convenience store robbery. 1

  • Official

    A single song from start to finish, credited by Rockstar in the video’s description: “Love Is a Long Road” by Tom Petty (1989). 12
