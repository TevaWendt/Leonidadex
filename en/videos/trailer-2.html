Trailer 2 for GTA 6

Released on May 6, 2025 along with the character and region pages: Jason and Lucia’s story, from the Leonida Keys to Vice City, over “Hot Together” by the Pointer Sisters.

  • Official

    The official summary: an easy score goes wrong, and the duo ends up on the dark side of the sunniest place in America. 1

  • Official

    On the same day, Rockstar releases pages for eight characters, the six regions of Leonida and dozens of screenshots. 123

  • Official

    Main song credited by Rockstar: “Hot Together” by the Pointer Sisters (1986); short car radio snippets also play, noted by the press. 123

Frequently asked questions

What’s the music in Trailer 2 for GTA 6?
“Hot Together” by the Pointer Sisters (1986), credited by Rockstar; the press also noted short car radio snippets: Zenglen, Wang Chung and Tammy Wynette.
Which characters appear in Trailer 2?
Jason and Lucia, Cal Hampton and Brian Heder in the Keys, and Lucia facing the police, grenade launcher on her shoulder. Each character’s page lists the moments.
