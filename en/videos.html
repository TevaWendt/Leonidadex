Questions

Frequently asked questions

How many official GTA 6 videos are there?
Four: Trailer 1 (December 4, 2023), Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025), An Extended Look (August 27, 2026) and the launch trailer (October 15, 2026). Each one has its own page, where you can watch it.
Is there a GTA 6 Trailer 3?
Rockstar hasn’t released any video called “Trailer 3”. Its latest video, on October 15, 2026, is the launch trailer; before it came Trailer 1, Trailer 2 and An Extended Look.
What’s the music in the GTA 6 launch trailer?
“Flower in a Hurricane”, a new single by the duo Justice with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. The song is also on GTA VI: The Album, the game’s official album.
How long is the GTA 6 Extended Look?
About 26 minutes, according to Rockstar’s announcement. It aired on August 27, 2026, first on Netflix and then on YouTube.
What’s the music in Trailer 2 for GTA 6?
“Hot Together” by the Pointer Sisters (1986), credited by Rockstar; the press also noted short car radio snippets: Zenglen, Wang Chung and Tammy Wynette.
What’s the music in Trailer 1 for GTA 6?
“Love Is a Long Road” by Tom Petty, from the album Full Moon Fever (1989), credited by Rockstar in the video’s description.
