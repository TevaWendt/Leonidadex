06

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does GTA 6 cost?
€79.99 for the Standard Edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate Edition on the PlayStation Store in France, as of October 16, 2026. Retailers set their own prices, especially for the boxed version.
How much is the GTA 6 collector’s box?
$399.99 on the US Rockstar Store, according to the store itself. In France, the press reports it at €399.99; shipping isn’t free.
Is the game included in the collector’s box?
No: Rockstar states that the game, Grand Theft Auto VI, is sold separately. The box only contains its eleven items.
What’s in the GTA 6 collector’s box?
A Macca the Gator figure, Oakley Frogskins sunglasses, a New Era 9FORTY cap, a crossbody bag, a magnetic mirror, a shot glass, a swizzle spoon, a keychain, nine pins, nine stickers and a double-sided poster with the map of Leonida.
When does the collector’s box arrive?
The Rockstar Store ships it from November 19, 2026, the game’s release day. It’s a limited edition, up for pre-order while supplies last, one per person.
Can you buy the collector’s box in France?
Yes: the Rockstar Store ships to France, Belgium, Switzerland and Luxembourg, among others. Shipping costs are shown at checkout; taxes may be added outside the US and the UK.
How much is the GTA 6 DualSense controller?
€84.99, suggested price, for each of the two limited-edition controllers, the black one and the white one, from November 19, 2026. In France, the black one is sold only on direct.playstation.com.
