Clothing and style

Hairstyles, tattoos, outfits and accessories: everything that changes how Jason and Lucia look, in three expandable lists, with the announced collections and the shops Rockstar has shown.

No GTA VI prices have been published: each row shows its status, and GTA V and GTA Online prices stay in their benchmark column. The collections described aren’t a list of items you can buy.

Official sources

What Rockstar has said and shown

Four stages, each with its status: the screenshots, the editions, the Extended Look, then Rob Nelson’s interviews. No prices, no item lists.

  1. May 6, 2025Seen in media

    Official screenshots: what Jason and Lucia wear

    “Leonida” print tank top, chain, watch and cap for Jason; high bun, hoop earrings and sunglasses for Lucia. The images show items, not a store or a price.

  2. June 24, 2026Official

    Editions: three shops and two collections

    The Ultimate Edition opens Stock 305 (streetwear by Stockyard), Sara’s Unisex Salon (signature hairstyles, Jason’s beard, Lucia’s makeup and nails) and Electric Fang Tattoo (over 50 tattoos designed by FAILE), and adds the Vice City Style and Goodtime Gear collections. The Vintage Vice City Pack gives Jason a pastel linen suit and a retro hairstyle, and Lucia a red sequin mini dress and curls. Rockstar also lists the basic options: hairstyle, facial hair, makeup, nails, and arm, leg and back tattoos.

  3. August 27, 2026Seen in media

    Extended Look: the in-game wardrobe

    Lucia picks an outfit before a night out while Jason comments; the duo robs gas stations with bandanas over their faces. Nothing is named or priced.

  4. August 28, 2026Official

    Rob Nelson: thousands of outfits, salons everywhere

    Speaking to Famitsu (translated on September 9), then to GamingBible (September 10): “We have thousands of outfits”, earned in missions or bought, “countless clothing stores and hair salons”; you can cut your hair at the safehouse, with limited choices. To Dazed (September 6), stylist Jillian Carr: Vice City “is built around vanity”.

The three lists below sort all of this row by row, with each row’s status and source, followed by GTA V and GTA Online haircuts, tattoos and clothing with their prices, shown as benchmarks.

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Can you change your hairstyle in GTA 6?
Yes: at the safehouse with a limited choice, or at a salon for many more cuts, according to Rob Nelson. Sara’s Unisex Salon (Ultimate Edition) adds signature hairstyles, Jason’s beard, and Lucia’s makeup and nails. Prices not published; in GTA V, a haircut costs $20 to $450.
Can you get tattoos in GTA 6?
Yes. Rockstar mentions the arms, legs and back; Electric Fang Tattoo (Ultimate Edition) offers more than 50 tattoos designed by FAILE. Prices not published; in GTA V, a design costs $50 to $550 and can be removed for free.
How many outfits are in GTA 6?
“Thousands,” according to Rob Nelson, bought in stores or earned in missions, in “countless” stores. Stock 305 (Ultimate Edition) sells exclusive streetwear looks. No prices have been published.
Are there accessories (glasses, jewelry, watches) in GTA 6?
The official screenshots show some: sunglasses, Lucia’s hoop earrings, Jason’s chain, watch and cap; the Goodtime Gear collection includes accessories. None are sold separately so far.
What does the Vintage Vice City Pack include for style?
A pastel linen suit and a retro hairstyle for Jason, a red sequin mini dress and curls for Lucia, plus a tropical pattern for most weapons, the Stanier ’55 and the Shore Court garage. Only for purchases made before November 20, 2026.
