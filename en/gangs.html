Gangs and factions · GTA VI

The gangs and factions of GTA VI

Ambrosia bikers, a Southside gang, Raul’s robbers, the Keys drug runners and law enforcement: the groups Rockstar has shown, and what’s still to be confirmed.

Each page says where the group’s name comes from: a Rockstar text, a patch or a sign visible on screen, or a reading by players. Showing a group doesn’t tell you whether it’ll be an ally or a target: its role is still to be discovered. Understanding statuses.

The 6 pages

Motorcycle club

Final Chapter MC

The refinery provides the work, the club supplies the rest.

Ambrosia’s motorcycle club, founded in 1982 according to its black and gold patches: the local biker gang mentioned in the region’s official introduction.

Official sources

What Rockstar has shown

No Rockstar text lists the gangs. Still, patches, a sign, an edition text and an interview sketch out the landscape.

  1. December 4, 2023Seen in media

    Trailer 1

    A Vice-Dale Police Department car and the Leonard County Sheriff’s Office: the first readable law enforcement names.

  2. May 6, 2025Official

    Regions and characters

    According to Rockstar, in Ambrosia the refinery provides the jobs and the local biker gang takes care of the rest; in the screenshot, the vests read “Final Chapter”. Raul recruits robbers, Jason works for the Keys drug runners.

  3. May 6, 2025Seen in media

    Trailer 2

    An armored police van faces Lucia around 1:59; around 1:25, Brian’s boatyard is loading cargo at night.

  4. June 24, 2026Official

    Ultimate Edition

    First gang named in an official text: the PTT Youngin$ of Southside, whose hideout you raid before getting away. Another screenshot shows a patrol boat marked Vice City Police.

  5. August 27, 2026Seen in media

    Extended Look

    A delivery for Boobie at Venture Apartments turns into a police raid; Raul takes Jason and Lucia into his crew; Brian hands them a job over a meal.

  6. August 28, 2026Official

    Famitsu

    Rob Nelson (Rockstar North): you sneak into gang hideouts to steal money or drugs, which the map puts a number on ahead of time; the drugs get sold to a fence. No witness or alarm, no wanted level.

Here, a name “read on screen” comes from a patch, a marking or a sign: that doesn’t mean Rockstar has introduced it.

05

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions people type into a search engine, with short, dated answers.

Which gangs are confirmed in GTA VI?
Two groups have a name in official media: the Final Chapter MC, read on the vests in an Ambrosia screenshot, and Southside’s PTT Youngin$, named in the Ultimate Edition text. San4San is only a reading by players.
Can you attack gang hideouts?
Yes, according to Rob Nelson (Famitsu): you break into a hideout to steal money or drugs. The map shows what it holds; the drugs get sold to a fence.
Who are the Ambrosia bikers?
The Final Chapter MC, founded in 1982 going by its patches: as Rockstar puts it, the local biker gang provides pretty much everything the refinery doesn’t.
How does the wanted level work?
A crime is only reported if someone sees it or an alarm goes off, and the stars go up, but not excessively (Rob Nelson). Glasses or a haircut aren’t enough: the police know your clothes.
Can you join Raul’s crew?
In the story, yes: the Extended Look shows Raul taking Jason and Lucia into his crew after a successful getaway. What happens next hasn’t been published.

Sources and statuses

Every claim on this page has a status and comes from a page checked on the date shown. The Ultimate Edition text and Rob Nelson’s comments were reread on October 3, 2026 in the sources that reproduce them.

Official

A page or video published by Rockstar (GTA VI site, Newswire, trailers, Extended Look), or statements by a Rockstar representative reported by a named outlet, with the date.

Seen in media

What an official image or clip shows without any Rockstar text saying it: a trailer shot, a screenshot, a moment from the Extended Look, with the timestamp when known.

Community identification

A match made by players, a wiki or a fan site (real place, actor, county). Quoted as such, never presented as confirmed.

To be confirmed

A question with no published answer: price, purchase, access, role in the story. We write it down so we don’t guess it.
