The rules

How to earn points

  1. A right answer

    Each right answer in a daily game earns you 10 points, as soon as the game is finished.

  2. A flawless game

    5 out of 5 in a daily game: 20 extra points.

  3. Today’s challenge

    One challenge a day, the same for everyone: 50 extra points if you complete it.

  4. Free play

    It earns nothing: it’s for practice, with no limit and without touching your points.

03

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on this device only, in your browser’s storage. No account, no cookie, nothing is sent. On another device or another browser, you start from scratch.
Do my earlier Guess the Location games count?
Yes. Daily games you’ve already finished in Guess the Location count towards your points, your streak and your badges, without changing your scores.
Why doesn’t free play earn points?
So everyone moves forward the same way: only the daily games count, and they’re the same for everyone. Free play is for practice.
Where do the questions come from?
From the site’s checked data: the screenshots Rockstar sorts by region, dated announcements from Rockstar and the press, and the six radio stations Rockstar presented. Nothing comes from leaks.
