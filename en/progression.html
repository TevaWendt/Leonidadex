Back up or transfer your tracking

Everything you check stays in this browser. Export a file to keep it, or to get it back on another device: garage, arsenal, spotted places, documented content, saved calculator calculations, map markers and drawings, ranking.

Before importing, here’s what the file contains

    “Merge” adds the file’s checkmarks to yours. “Replace” puts the file in place of your checkmarks, section by section; anything the file doesn’t contain is left untouched.
