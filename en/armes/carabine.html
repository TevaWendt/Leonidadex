What the artwork shows

A modern carbine with a telescoping stock, visible in several action sequences.

In plain words

On Leonidakit, the Carbine belongs to the assault rifles category. We’ve seen it, but it hasn’t been named yet: this page keeps a descriptive name. It’s a long gun: in the game, you carry it on your back or in your hand, and the number of long guns you can take with you is limited. The closest real-world model is the AR-15 / M4, based on the images. It uses rifle ammo. Estimated range: medium to long.

Damage, fire rate, accuracy, recoil, Ammu-Nation price and unlock level will be measured in the game and added to this page.

Its place in a GTA

In a GTA, the assault rifle is the one you keep in your hands for serious missions: range, fire rate and a big magazine.
