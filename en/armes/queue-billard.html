Overview

A pool cue, a melee weapon tied to Leonida’s leisure spots.

In plain words

On Leonidakit, the Pool cue belongs to the melee category. Sighted in official images with no label: this page names it by its type and its likely real-world model. It’s a melee weapon: you carry it on you and it needs no ammo and no reloading. It’s an everyday object: an American pool cue. Its estimated range is close quarters.

What’s still missing (damage, effective range, price, attachments) will come from in-game measurements, never from estimates.

In the series

In a GTA, a melee weapon is for when you want to cause trouble without firing a shot, or when you have nothing else at hand.
