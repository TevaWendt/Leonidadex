What the official media show

A pair of engraved revolvers, one for Jason, the other for Lucia, described by Rockstar as inspired by the Vercetti Estate in Vice City. Palm-tree grips, name engravings and a high-performance scope.

In plain words

Pistols category: that’s where Hawk & Little Morgan sits in our database. The weapon is officially named: the match with a real-world model, on the other hand, is still the community’s reading. It’s a sidearm: kept on you and invisible once put away, unlike long guns, which can be seen. The exact number you can carry is still to be confirmed. The chosen match is the Smith & Wesson 629, a reading of the artwork and not information from the studio. Ammo: pistol ammo. Estimated range: medium. It’s featured in the Ultimate Edition material.

This page will be filled in at launch: combat stats, price, available attachments and where to buy it.

This weapon type in the series

The series’ pistols and revolvers differ in fire rate, capacity and power: small calibers shoot fast, big revolvers hit hard.
