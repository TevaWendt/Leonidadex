In plain words

On Leonidakit, the Hammer belongs to the melee category. Sighted in the official images, with no label: the page names it by its type and its likely real-world model. It belongs with the melee weapons, the ones you use up close, with no ammo. Its type: carpenter’s hammer. Estimated range: close quarters.

What’s still missing (damage, effective range, price, attachments) will come from in-game measurements, never from estimates.
