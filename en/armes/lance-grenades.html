What the images tell us

Lucia holds it during the police chase in the second trailer, just as a vehicle explodes behind her. The big cylinder sticking out under the barrel leaves no doubt: a Milkor MGL-style revolver grenade launcher, the return of the series’ most spectacular heavy weapon.

In plain words

Grenade launcher is filed under special in our database. The weapon appears in material published by Rockstar, with no name: the one shown here describes its type and will change to match the first official mention. A long gun, so it’s bulky: it takes one of the two available slots, on your back or in hand. The closest real-world model is Milkor MGL, based on the images. It uses grenades. Estimated range: medium.

This page will be filled in at launch: combat stats, price, available attachments and where to buy it.
